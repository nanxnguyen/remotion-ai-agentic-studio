# Ray Tạo Video Tâm Lý Học — video psychology Shorts production

## Goal and operating contract

Produce **original, useful, visually expressive Vietnamese psychology/human-behavior Shorts**, not copies of reference videos or interchangeable AI slideshow templates. Build **playable MP4** when the user requests actual video, not just an outline or storyboard. Distinguish what was actually produced from what is only planned. The user wants high-quality work and eventual legitimate monetization, but no earnings, views or YPP approval are guaranteed.

**This is a skills-first plugin.** It orchestrates currently available tools and connected external apps (such as GitHub, Google Drive, Vbee via an authorized runner, and local Remotion/FFmpeg). It does **not** by itself host a render server, hold Vbee credentials, publish to YouTube, or give automatic access to another app. Check available capabilities each session; when a needed integration is not connected or authorized, say so and produce the most complete useful local artifact without claiming success.

## Default brief when user says “làm video mới”

- Language: natural Vietnamese. Target: 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/yuv420p, AAC stereo 48 kHz. Duration **30–45s preferred**, adjusted to real narration; **never pad** to arbitrary length. If explicitly requesting 50–60s, honor duration when justified.
- Three adaptable series formats (do not make every episode a list): **A Psychology Facts** (4–6 grounded insights), **B Psychology Mini Story** (one character, cause→decision→consequence), **C Psychology Explained** (one real-world example→mechanism→actionable takeaway). Use all three across the slate, avoid near-duplicate emotional mechanisms; see strategy reference.
- **Opening micro-CTA / viewer challenge:** every video has a **unique 1–4s intro/hook** that actively invites the viewer to notice, choose, predict, or consider a real dilemma, or provokes credible curiosity. Draft 3 distinct candidates and check against recent spoken intros and visuals; no fixed sentence shared between videos. The hook must be complete by second 4 as measured from genuine narration, even when the body continues longer. Read `references/opening-hook-variation-1-to-4s.md` for hard gates. Never substitute likes/subs pressure for a viewer challenge.
- Thumbnail and titles: readable on phone, credible, no false scientific certainty or invented authority. Frame 0–1s shows a *specific contradiction or relatable action*, aligned to the distinct 1–4s spoken opening. Meaningful changes every 2–5s as story needs. No generic greetings and no mandatory 10-scene count.
- Stick figures: original vector character designs, expressive and limb-attached acting, animated props, legible scene-specific staging. Dark/white/amber/cyan is an optional *identity palette*, **not** a mandatory cloned layout. Vary cause, setting, framing, gestures, typography and end message.
- CTA can ask viewers to **thả tim + đăng ký** near end, but must not replace the actual takeaway, pressure people, fake an incentive or obscure the scene.

## Workflow — execute in order, no silent skips

1. **Inspect request/assets** and latest current repo rules, if repo used. When provided a Drive MP4, inspect that exact file or available local copy, not a similarly named result. If only asked to brainstorm, deliver ideas instead of rendering.
2. **Originality scan:** compare at least last ten known topics/manifests in `Videos-shorts` when accessible. Explicitly check for overlap with **#11 “Lớn rồi, không cần làm vừa lòng tất cả nữa”** and **#27 “6 dấu hiệu bạn luôn cố làm hài lòng người khác”** for boundaries/people-pleasing themes. Same style or narrator is fine; copying the same plot, claim order or shot sequence is not. If comparison is incomplete, mark unverified.
3. **Evidence gate:** if fact-based psychology, separate **established finding**, **qualified generalization**, **illustrative fictional scenario**, and **opinion**. Verify through trustworthy health/psychology/academic sources where needed. Never claim to diagnose someone from gestures/sleep/eye movement, never promise to “đọc vị” everyone, never invent study statistics or label a person narcissistic, depressed, psychopathic etc based on a short. Include a concrete, non-coercive actionable takeaway. For fictional drama, call it a story rather than scientific fact.
4. **Script lock + mandatory intro diversity:** draft 3 **substantively different** topic-specific opening hooks; select one that challenges/invites the viewer to think, compare its *exact spoken words and visual action* with at least the 10 most recent available video intros, record its family and why it is unique. **Never recycle a hook sentence across different episodes.** Target an action-driven intro of **1–4 seconds**; after Vbee TTS, verify actual start/end duration and revise/re-render if it does not fit. Store `intro_hook`/ledger alongside timecoded spoken script and storyboard whose actions correspond to each spoken claim. Approve on clarity, novelty and accurate claims before TTS. Use canonical script text; avoid automatic narration paraphrases.
5. **Real Vbee casting:** select **by script**, never force Thanh Long. Verify real voice codes/entitlements and commercial terms; audition 1–3 clips on the same passage when possible. Useful **confirmed catalogue candidates** include **Thanh Mai Pro** `sg_female_thanhmai_yt_24k-pre`, **Thảo Trinh** `sg_female_thaotrinh_full_48k-fhg`, **Tường Vy** `sg_female_tuongvy_call_44k-fhg`, **Hà Chi** `hn_female_hachi_book_22k-vc`, and **Minh Quân Pro** `hn_male_minhquan_yt_24k-pre`; confirm active status at use time. Voice name is not sufficient evidence of API access. Save Vbee `voiceCode`, real generated MP3, audio hash, run/artifact provenance and license status in `voice-cast.json`. For #27, historical V2 preferred Thanh Mai Pro; **do not blindly carry her to different episodes**.
6. **Audio-derived timeline:** measure real final voice via ffprobe; preserve natural breaths and avoid >0.65s unintended gaps. Use a **word/phrase alignment from this exact MP3** and manual correction when possible; do not confuse scene-based proportional estimates with verified word-level alignment. Full voice must match the spoken captions word-for-word; descriptions/titles remain visually distinct from captions.
7. **Remotion-native visual production:** use real React/Remotion `interpolate`, `spring`, `AbsoluteFill`, `Sequence`, SVG rigs and animated original art; source should be reproducible. Check repo `nanxnguyen/remotion-ai-agentic-studio` latest `AGENTS.md`, video registry, pipeline and relevant production standards before changing existing repo. Keep voice/frames synchronized; no static-slide montage disguised by camera zoom. Distinguish native Remotion from Pillow/FFmpeg-only renders accurately.
8. **Comprehensive QA:** run `scripts/verify_shorts.py` and `scripts/check_hook.py` where available, ffprobe, full FFmpeg decode; inspect **rendered** first **4s**, confirm the chosen spoken intro genuinely lasts **1–4s** and differs from previous intros, then inspect all scene boundaries, phone-size subtitles, signs/counters, prop contact, clipping, CTA and final frame; listen to complete mixed audio. Check `06/06` style counters never show `08/06`; CTA must not overlap feet or platform controls. An automated pass alone does not establish visual/artistic success or 100% word sync.
9. **YouTube safety gate:** verify originality, audio/image/music commercial rights, sensitivity/ad-suitability, intended audience (stick figures ≠ automatically Made for Kids), synthetic content disclosure if realism requires, honest thumbnail/copy. Save `youtube-safety-review-Vx.json`. A non-original or improperly licensed asset blocks release. `REVIEW` until every mandatory check has evidence and user approval where required.
10. **Versioned delivery:** follow Google Drive root `Videos-shorts` ID `1rtJF9zDWuFZ8_6IixdMew3WCZj8415-D`; list/paginate folders, reuse one canonical numbered folder per topic, create new only with latest max+1, never overwrite V1/V2. Upload MP4, source project, original Vbee, voice cast, subtitle timestamps, thumbnail, metadata, QA and safety report, then verify exact Drive IDs/bytes/MIME/parent. If fully QA-approved, archive MP4 to GitHub **draft** release only when permitted and verified; never claim an Action artifact is permanent GitHub release. **Never publish to YouTube/TikTok without explicit permission.**
11. **Deliver**: true working MP4 link and Drive folder; metadata and factual QA; explain remaining REVIEW blockers in Vietnamese. For analysis-only tasks, deliver a prioritized diagnostic and upgrade plan; do not claim rendered output.

## Hard stops and integrity rules

- No real Vbee audio → do not label audio as Vbee. No playable verified MP4 → do not say “đã dựng xong”. No actual word alignment → do not claim 100% sync. No manual watching/listening → no FINAL claim. Missing or unverified commercial rights → don't publish.
- Do not imply YouTube algorithm boosts a video by using fixed secret timing, nor guarantee monetization, views or a 30-day income. Organic experiments and audience analytics are the basis for changes.
- Do not automatically reuse voice, visuals, “6 dấu hiệu” plots or fixed CTA arrangement. A common branded style is allowed; each episode needs distinct intellectual and visual value.
