## Distinguish four different risks

1. **Community Guidelines violations** → removal/strikes. Graphic abuse, exploitative material, threats, harmful medical misinformation etc.
2. **Ad-suitability limitation** → limited/no ads without necessarily removing video; context and thumbnails matter.
3. **YPP channel monetization/inauthentic content** → repetitive, mass-produced, unoriginal or minimally varied episodes, reused clips or assets. Shared series look, AI use, frequent uploads and consistent Vbee voice are **not alone** disqualifying.
4. **Low distribution** → often retention/competition/demand; no evidence of policy penalties from low view counts alone.

## Episode gate

- Check recent 10 episodes if available; originality of topic, mechanism, script, examples, plot, images, choreography, not merely changed names.
- Original/licensed visuals, music, SFX and voice with documented commercial terms. TTS API access ≠ blanket right to use every tier/clone commercially. No third-party Shorts frames/audio reused without permission.
- Psychology claims **scientifically accurate** and qualified; no sensational "9 mẹo đọc vị ai cũng đúng", no fabricated diagnosis, credentials, case histories, statistics or misleading therapy claims.
- Themes of grief, manipulation, infidelity and emotional pain can be discussed without graphic descriptions, sexualized minors, exploitative child harm, glorification of self-harm, discrimination, frightening false emergencies or exploitative thumbnails.
- Animation is **not automatically child-directed**. Choose audience from real subject/intended viewers; do not intentionally misuse the setting.
- AI animated fictional figures usually aren't realistic synthetic depictions; evaluate truthful disclosure when realistic individuals/events/voice imitation are involved. A label alone is not an ad ban.
- Brief CTA like/subscribe allowed; no fake engagement, spam, coercive like-for-reward, paid views/subs or manipulative deadlines.
- Title/thumbnail accurately reflect the exact episode (not fake clinical authority or "100% proven" promises). If a mini-story is dramatized, identify it as illustrative/fictional as needed.

Use `references/templates/youtube-safety-review.json` and statuses PASS / REVISE / BLOCK / NOT_CHECKED. **Block publishing** when commercial rights unverified, serious policy violation, or major misleading information. Mark REVIEW when visual/voice/audio/timing can't be independently checked. Manual approval before public posting.
