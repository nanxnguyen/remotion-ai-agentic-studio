# Ray Psychology — Series strategy and retention

## Positioning

**“Tâm lý con người qua những hành vi và câu chuyện khiến bạn phải suy ngẫm.”** Vietnamese entertainment-education about behavior, emotion, relationships, boundaries, empathy and evidence-backed practical tools. Audience: general adult/viewers seeking relatable explanations, not diagnostic service. Not automatically directed at children merely because visuals are stick figures.

## Three format families

| Format | Typical duration | Value proposition | Distinctive visual grammar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A Facts / Myth-check | 30–42s, 4–6 points | One topic with qualified, non-diagnostic observations | Icons→situations→counterexample; avoid num-only slides |
| B Mini Story | 35–50s | One protagonist makes a consequential psychological choice | Character acting, relatable conflict, before/after agency |
| C Explained | 35–55s | Explain one mechanism with a specific real-world example | Cause-effect visual, split screen and actionable demonstration |

Don't enforce a 7/5/4/4 or 35/25/20/20 quota as official YouTube policy. Rotate topics for novelty instead of pumping one format. For a slate of 20: optional categories family psychology (7), behavior (5), boundaries/healing (4), empathy/ethical choice (4), only as planning inspiration.

## Retention blueprint (not a guaranteed algorithm)

- Frame 0–1s: show a clear social dilemma (e.g., a character volunteering despite an overflowing calendar), not a channel logo on black.
- **0–4s: finish a fresh topic-specific viewer mini-challenge/curiosity intro in 1–4s of real measured Vbee speech.** Make opening pattern, spoken words, visuals and actual question distinct from previous uploads; compare at least 10 available openings. Never repeat a stock “Bạn có biết…” or “Thử xem…” starter. See `opening-hook-variation-1-to-4s.md`.
- ~4–6s: develop what matters and answer the challenge at an earned beat; do not stretch hook past 4s.
- 5–12s: specific scene and practical stakes.
- Middle: each 2–5s beat adds an action, counterpoint, evidence or different illustrative behavior; purposeful cuts over filler shakes.
- Final 5–9s: deliver the insight and actual usable example; CTA *after* value, brief and unobtrusive.
- Trim empty silence without unnatural pace. Script length from measured real voice.

## Anti-duplication story ledger

For every proposed episode write:
- `claim_or_conflict`: what is new?
- `psychological_mechanism`: different from recent releases?
- `story_structure`: why this isn’t the same cause/response/conclusion?
- `visual_signature`: distinctive character action/prop/camera changes?
- `scientific_support`: what sources back claims?
- `format`: A/B/C, and why this format suits the topic?

Compare against 10 recent items when available, including known #11 and #27 people-pleasing themes. **Not acceptable novelty:** swapping male/female protagonist, changing name, backdrop color, generic “you may be X” to “6 signs X,” or simply switching Vbee voice.

## Suggested video experiments

- *A*: “3 điều dễ bị hiểu sai về việc đặt ranh giới” — contrast myths vs lived examples, not diagnosis.
- *B*: “Lần đầu cô ấy nói không với sếp” — one work scenario with competing values, clear decision.
- *C*: “Tại sao trả lời ngay khiến bạn dễ nhận lời quá mức?” — explain situational pressure with careful qualifiers and actionable pause.
- Rotate outside boundaries: friendships, family misunderstandings, self-compassion, attention, cognitive biases, communication (careful not to turn speculation into universal fact).

Measure **shown in feed**, viewed-vs-swiped away, average view duration/percentage, comments and subscriber conversion in YouTube Studio after publishing. Compare with same-channel baselines and iterate; avoid fabricated benchmarks or “shadowban” conclusions.
