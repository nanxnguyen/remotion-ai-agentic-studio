# Cinematic direction — stick figures with expressive acting

**Character continuity:** fixed anatomy ratios and line quality, head-to-body attachment, consistent hand-prop contact, walk-cycle planted foot, matching screen direction on scene cuts. Five essential emotional poses: hopeful, waiting, anxious, defeated, resolved.

**Scene changes:** every shot conveys a new idea or emotion; mix extreme-wide, medium, close, phone-screen insert, POV, shoulder reaction, symbolic negative-space. Prefer match cuts and carefully motivated cuts. Preserve focal point across shot boundaries; avoid repeated generic zoom. Let the viewer read micro-expressions and holds.

**Story #12 motif:** phone's amber notification → dim/empty phone → amber dot fading → character sets phone face-down. The final act must be *choosing oneself*, not merely loneliness. 18 scene beats, 42.6s preview; actual speech 38.635s, allow end hold. On-screen captions 1–2 lines with safe area and Vietnamese glyph checks.

**Voice:** direct each utterance with intention, stress, breath and phrase-final emotion. Generate separate takes for hook and emotional apex where tooling permits. Correct mechanical delivery; never certify human-like voice without listening.

**Audio:** fragile low-register piano for waiting; restrained tension at realization; small hopeful chord shift at choice. Mix music clearly below speech. Verify final export's separate voice presence, not just an audio stream.

**QA:** inspect frame 0, every scene boundary ±12 frames, climax, last seconds. Check no blinks/flash frames, silhouette consistency, voice audibility, clipped words, mismatch. Full pipeline gate evidence before calling final.
