# DUAL CTA + LUXURY STORYBOARD — future NEW_FILM only

**ChatGPT Ray Phim Kể Chuyện plugin v0.3.3 is already updated.** This repo skill is the source mirror. Respect explicit user overrides, current canonical Ray QA gates, and the NO GITHUB RELEASES policy.

- **1–3s opening challenge/curiosity:** write a specific line for that film (for horror, e.g. “Nếu đủ can đảm… hãy xem đến cuối!”); use real suspenseful footage with original character/props, not unmotivated black-card filler.
- **3–5s closing like+subscribe/follow:** write a different, story-relevant line after twist/payoff; do not use one canned CTA for every movie.
- **Storyboard BEFORE render:** draw an independent 9:16 visual storyboard card for the **opening CTA** and another for **closing CTA**, with real concept art, action/character continuity, distinct **upper headline** and **lower Vbee narration subtitle**, color/type hierarchy, planned SFX/fades, frame/time budget. Expand the 4×3/4×4 grid rather than omitting story-action frames.
- **Visual design:** horror defaults toward Mini Horror × Luxury Cinematic (inky dark/teal, wet glass or relevant prop, champagne gold, restrained crimson). Nonhorror color/diction adapted to its genre. Preserve **exact approved storyboard/key-art visual hierarchy** in the final intro/outro; no generic title panels or distorted text.
- **Copy roles:** top = 2–7-word scene-specific cinematic headline, not duplicate of voice; bottom = exact real Vbee narration subtitle, 1–2 lines, safe for phone viewing.
- **Timing:** allocate both inside the user-approved runtime unless the user explicitly approves extending the film; avoid overlap with 0–6s story hook; preserve the essential reveal before outro.
- **Voice/QA:** create authentic Vbee Batch CTA voice(s), measure real durations and SHA-256, never cut syllables. Inspect rendered MP4 actual opening/closing frames and listen to spoken CTA, check against storyboard approved art/text and audio sync; no timecodes/grid/badges in MP4. Do not claim FINAL on unrun gates.
- **Scope:** do NOT revisit old Drive films or delete/replace historical assets unless expressly requested.
