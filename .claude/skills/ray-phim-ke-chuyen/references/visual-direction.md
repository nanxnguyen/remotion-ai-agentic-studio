# Visual direction — Ray cinematic story film

## Style tokens

- Canvas: vertical 9:16, mobile-first; typical target 1080×1920 @30fps for new production, keep original FPS/dimensions on updates.
- Charcoal `#08090C`, deep blue-black `#11151E`, cream/white `#F5F5F4`, warning yellow `#FFD429`, crimson `#F42F3E`, secondary gray `#85878A`.
- Desaturated photographs, textured lighting, dark corners, subtle rain, tungsten practicals, analog grain, scratches and light bloom. *No gore required.*
- Typography: Vietnamese-safe condensed bold (e.g. Be Vietnam Pro/Roboto Condensed with available bold weights); word emphasis is primarily yellow, red reserved for one danger/twist word. Programmatically overlay 1–2 blocks of captions; maintain safe margins.

## Visual prompting protocol

For each shot, specify: `character ID`, visual appearance, outfit continuity, environment, location within scene, shot type (wide/medium/insert/closeup), lens/framing, lighting, emotional beat, continuity prop, negative space for captions, and safe 9:16 composition. Keep reference face/style sheets consistent. Visual prompts should ask for **NO baked-in text, NO logos, NO watermarks**; overlay text through Remotion. Text-to-image generated timestamps, captions and small signs are prone to inaccuracies; remake in code if important.

Example prompt (fictional hotel):

> Vertical 9:16 cinematic psychological mystery film still. Vietnamese woman, age mid-20s, wet black shoulder-length hair, dark charcoal coat, weary eyes, consistent identity across shots. Old hotel hallway, wallpaper peeling, warm tungsten practical lights, shallow depth of field, noir shadows, restrained red accent, 35mm analog film grain, realistic still photography, place the subject left-center and preserve lower-center negative space for captions. No text, no embedded captions, no borders, no watermark.

## Retention without visual clutter

- 0–3s a specific visually striking hook with immediate question.
- Rotate meaningful shot functions: protagonist close-up, exterior establishing, hand/key insert, receptionist reaction, 4th-floor button, eerie hallway, frozen clock, polaroid, phone, mirror, new evidence, ending door.
- Prefer story-motivated transitions: cut on SFX, match cut between recurring props, slow push-in on reveals, slow hold after twist.
- 4×4 contact sheets are for storyboard review only. Render each final 9:16 image as its own scene with timing, motion, lighting, subtitle overlay and audio.
- Keep onscreen dialogue shorter than full narration; subtitles can be phrase-synchronized. Do not display tiny full transcripts in one card.

## QA

Verify same character face throughout, no sudden costume or hotel-location drift, legible Vietnamese, caption safe margins, no mirrored nonsensical text, key/clock/door number consistent, no abrupt black/frozen frames, actual scene and climax staging rather than 15 identical zooms.
