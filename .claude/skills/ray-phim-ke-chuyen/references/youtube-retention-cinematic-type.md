# YouTube Shorts retention + cinematic type system (Vietnamese mystery stories)

This is an **editorial and measurement framework**, not a guarantee of reach. Optimize for viewer satisfaction, truthful promises, story clarity and replay value; never use fabricated claims, manipulative clickbait or unrelated hashtags.

## Retention-driven story structure (55–65 seconds)

1. **0–2s: pattern interrupt + stakes**. First frame has a visual anomaly; begin narration immediately. The opening should make one specific, truthful promise that the payoff resolves. Example: "Máy ảnh của An chụp được tương lai... nhưng bức thứ tư báo trước cái chết của anh."
2. **2–12s: evidence**. Establish one understandable rule and demonstrate it (broken glass predicted then broken). Avoid logos, long intros and repetitive setup.
3. **12–30s: escalation**. Every 4–7s reveal a meaningful new clue or consequence; raise stakes through cause and effect rather than arbitrary jump cuts. Change shot size, motivated camera motion, lighting or SFX when story state changes.
4. **30–45s: point of no return**. Sound drop or short silence followed by the key reveal. Keep spatial continuity and make the audience understand what is dangerous.
5. **45–60s: earned payoff + resonant final beat**. Explain the core mechanism with a twist grounded in earlier clues. End on a meaningful image/SFX; optional *natural* question in description or pinned comment, not a spoken request that undermines the ending. Never tack on "Đây là câu chuyện hư cấu" to the narrated ending.

**Narrative QA gate**: story must have HOOK, BUILD, CLIMAX and PAYOFF. A cliffhanger may leave future consequences open, but must resolve the central promise of this episode. No bait-and-switch.

## Typography / motion design system

- **Mobile safe area**: 9:16, 1080×1920 master when feasible, at least 720×1280. Keep essential text away from bottom 22%, top 12%, and right-side interaction rail. Prefer a readable left-aligned lower-middle title panel with measured safe margins.
- **Hierarchy**: one cinematic headline (max 2 lines, 3–6 words), one small kicker (e.g. "CHƯƠNG 02 / 03:00 AM") and optional one-line supporting subtitle. Do not fill the screen with full narration; voice carries detail. If word-by-word subtitles are used, render separately in 2–5 word chunks.
- **Fonts**: Vietnamese-complete sans family such as Noto Sans Display Condensed Black / Noto Sans Bold for headline, Noto Sans Medium for microcopy. Render actual UTF-8 with tested accents. Prefer near-white (#F4F0E8), amber (#E4B76D) for discoveries and dark crimson (#B93E49) for threats. Avoid neon yellow walls of text.
- **Treatment**: subtle gradient scrim, optical tracking, soft shadow, 1–2px keyline, filmic grain, hairline divider, chapter number and a small progress indicator. 1 dominant emphasis per scene. Use red sparingly, especially for final reveal.
- **Motion**: headline fades/raises in over 8–12 frames with subtle blur resolving to sharp; no bounce, spinning text or distracting kinetic typography. Slow Ken Burns/2.5D camera drift is motivated by the clue. Cut or dissolve on beats, but don't crossfade subtitles into unreadable doubles. For the shutter, use a restrained 2–3 frame flash and transient sound, not excessive strobing.
- **Voice**: regenerate Vbee from the *approved revised script* when lines change; never keep old audio under newly rewritten captions. Align each line to its slot based on measured MP3 duration; expand total runtime rather than cut off words.

## Publish and measure (no vanity-metric guarantees)

- Package 2–3 truthful title variants and a high-contrast first-frame cover. Example: "Bức ảnh thứ tư đã chụp cái chết của An | Truyện kinh dị #Shorts".
- Description: one compelling premise + concise series context + 2–3 directly relevant hashtags; use pinned comment to invite **specific** discussion about the twist.
- In YouTube Studio monitor **Stayed to watch / viewed vs swiped**, **Engaged views**, **Average view duration**, **Average percentage viewed**, and **retention dips/replays**. Compare against the channel's own recent Shorts rather than universal "viral" thresholds.
- If many swipe immediately, test an alternate first frame/first spoken sentence. If a sharp drop occurs mid-story, clarify the rule or remove a redundant scene. If payoff causes a drop, shorten the final explanation and make the final frame stronger.
- Test one creative variable at a time across subsequent uploads; log title, first frame, duration, hook variant and performance after 24h/7d. Do not fabricate metrics or imply YouTube upload occurred when it has not.
