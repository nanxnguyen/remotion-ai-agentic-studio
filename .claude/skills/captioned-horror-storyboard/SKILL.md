# STORYBOARD CINEMATIC CARDS V2 — DEFAULT REVIEW STYLE

**Use this as Ray Phim Kể Chuyện's default storyboard REVIEW presentation for future stories.** The approved visual model is the 2026-10-25 screenshot of a 4-column x 3-row cinematic-card storyboard (twelve vertical panels). This replaces the older unconditional white/yellow/red emphasis and unconditional 4x4 layout. Story beats and the color palette are authored to the actual story, not copied from the screenshot's bus/horror subject.

- **Card system:** default 4 columns × 3 rows for 50–60-second shorts (12 story beats); expand to 4×4 (16) or another deliberate count when the script needs more distinct actions. Cards have ~9:16 art, narrow even gaps, dark neutral review-canvas outside, precise alignment and strong readable hierarchy.
- **Each card includes:** (1) small top series/film identifier and shot number or progress line; (2) giant cinematic ALL-CAPS Vietnamese headline (2–7 words, dramatic but truthful), emphasizing 1–2 story keywords; (3) a distinct full-bleed story frame with readable faces, hands, props, and action; (4) bottom context line(s), usually 6–14 Vietnamese words, describing what changes in the story; (5) a frame time marker **below the card on review sheet only**.
- **Typography:** big bold condensed display font with clear accents, impactful scale, 1–2 highlighted phrases; contextual caption smaller, 1–2 lines. Place text on controlled gradients only as needed; never block crucial evidence with opaque rectangles. Always typeset Vietnamese independently with a real typography renderer (Pillow, HTML, Remotion), then proofread diacritics. Do not trust generated words from image models.
- **Adaptive palette is REQUIRED:** infer background, illumination, dominant color, 1–2 accent colors, and semantic typography colors from EACH story and its emotional stakes. The old red/black/yellow example is NOT fixed. e.g., icy blue/silver for cold isolation; marsh green/aged amber for folk curses; violet/slate for funerary dread; scarlet/coal for violent supernatural danger. These are optional examples, not a palette lookup table. Aim for strong contrast, coherent visual identity, and restrained highlight usage. Background of panel can vary by scene while the overall art direction stays consistent.
- **Dramaturgy:** visible anomaly within first 0–2s; progressive clue, threat, action, and twist; no repeated filler portrait. Mix POV, full figure, evidence inserts, supporting character, setting wide, reaction. Every panel must be legible even muted and correspond to a real shot/action.
- **Image accuracy:** every image individually composed for the corresponding story event, not repeated generic images, not recycled other movies' characters/props. Lock cast and setting details across frames.
- **Strict REVIEW/VIDEO separation:** review grid may contain huge headlines, timing below cards, shot numbers and story captions. The final MP4 contains **none of those review stamps, card frames, progress rails, burned review headlines or timecodes**. Film footage is produced from independent clean source images/video and receives only native Vbee-aligned subtitle overlays in safe areas. Never crop the storyboard contact sheet into the MP4.
- **QA:** verify 100% of cards show the correct action and text, readable mobile-size typography, Vietnamese accents, palette/contrast story fit, complete intended sequence and the user-approved shot duration. For final film, audit each rendered scene independently to prevent a black box obscuring the image.
