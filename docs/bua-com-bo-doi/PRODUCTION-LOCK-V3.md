## Creative lock
- **Story question:** Why has Dad started saying “Bố ăn trước nhé” after seven Sundays of waiting?
- **Core motif:** two white rice bowls, the second chair, the messages “Bố ăn trước nhé”.
- **Beginning (0–15s):** Father's unexpected reply, seven Sundays of the son's excuses, Dad returns the second bowl to the shelf.
- **Turning point (48–67s):** son notices the untouched bowl in Dad's old photo and erases “Con bận”.
- **Climax (74–82s):** “Con... về thật à?” / son gets the second bowl himself.
- **Payoff (82–86s):** following Sunday son waits for Dad.
- **CTA (86–90s):** subtle spoken & visible like + subscribe, only after emotional resolution.
- No illness, death, coercive crying, or invented tragic twist.

## Character / world bible
- **FATHER:** Vietnamese 63-year-old stick silhouette, slightly hunched back, short silver-hair cue, plain gray-brown shirt, slow deliberate hand motion, small smile, same face/scale every shot.
- **SON:** Vietnamese 29-year-old stick silhouette, dark office shirt with crossbody bag, initially tense shoulders and rushed keyboard movement; movement shifts to considered and decisive. Son's voice narrates in first person.
- **KITCHEN:** warm amber lamp (#EF9E69), tiny wooden table, two clearly identical white bowls (#F5F5F4), one empty chair, bowl shelf, rice cooker. Keep table, shelf and door axes consistent.
- **OFFICE:** near-black (#08090B) + cool gray-blue (#85878A); phone with native composited Vietnamese text, NOT AI lettering.
- **Primary visual staging:** father screen-left / empty chair screen-right; son approaches from screen-right on reunion shot. No abrupt spatial reversal.
- **Character motion:** head turns, torso tilt, hand/prop contact, foot plants, expressive holds; connected joint rig, minimum five credible emotion states; never substitute continuous zoom on still art for acting.
