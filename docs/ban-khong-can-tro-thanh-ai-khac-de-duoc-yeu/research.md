## 2. Universal Experience — Psychological Framing

### Core phenomenon: **Self-concealment for attachment**
- Attachment theory: Anxious attachment → hypervigilance to partner's needs → suppression of own needs
- "False self" (Winnicott) constructed to maintain connection
- Cost: Emotional exhaustion, resentment, identity diffusion
- Paradox: The more you mask, the less *actually known* you are → the love received feels unearned/unsafe
