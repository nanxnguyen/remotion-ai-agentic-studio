## Delivery Notes Per Shot (for voice-agent)

- **s1:** Curious, intimate. "xóa" slight emphasis. "quá" trailing off.
- **s2:** Wry smile in voice. "giả vờ" twice — first curious, second knowing. "chuyện nói" soft.
- **s3:** "Không buồn" deliberately flat (performed). "Nhạy cảm" — voice cracks warm.
- **s4:** Accelerating rhythm. "Giỏi đoán" crisp. "Dốt hỏi mình" — sudden quiet.
- **s5:** Full stop energy. Each phrase separated. "Khó" — long pause. "Trả lời" gentle.
- **s6:** **Split into s6a ("Rốt cuộc mình thật sự") + s6b ("thích điều gì?")**. s6a: breathy, searching. s6b: held, vulnerable. Real gap between clips.
- **s7:** Steady narrative. "Cố gắng" weight. "Giấu đi" — slight catch. "Chính mình" — grounded.
- **s8:** Reassuring teacher tone. "Điều chỉnh" clear. "Cả hai" — warm inclusion.
- **s9:** Expanding hope. "Vẫn còn chỗ" — pause after chỗ. "Con người thật" — tender. **60-frame ending hold** (mandatory per Gate A ticket pattern).
