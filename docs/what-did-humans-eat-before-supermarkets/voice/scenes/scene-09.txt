# Scene 09 narration — locked V3

Voice: Andrew (en-US-AndrewNeural)
Speed: 0.95

Today, food can travel thousands of miles before it reaches your plate.

A supermarket brings together products grown, raised, harvested, processed, stored, and transported through an enormous supply system.

Before modern food distribution, people depended much more directly on local resources and their knowledge of the environment.

That does not mean every ancient meal was harder to obtain, or that modern food systems are without problems.

The important difference is how people organize the work needed to get food.

The supermarket is a recent part of a much longer story.

The need to find, prepare, and share food connects people across time, even as the tools and systems around them change.
