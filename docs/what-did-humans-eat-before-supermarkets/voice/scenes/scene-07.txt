Food availability changes with the seasons.

A plant that provides fruit today may offer nothing several months later. Animals move, water levels change, and weather can affect the resources people depend on.

Different communities developed different responses.

Some moved as resources changed. Others combined many food sources or used methods of preservation when suitable.

There was no universal solution.

Nor should we imagine prehistoric life as either an effortless paradise or a constant disaster.

People faced uncertainty, but they also developed knowledge and strategies that helped them respond to the environments around them.
