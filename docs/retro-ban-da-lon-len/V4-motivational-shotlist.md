# Ban Da Lon Len — V4: Hành trình trưởng thành (shot direction)
Status: DRAFT / REVIEW. This revision is to correct *visual meaning*, not to change voice or script.

## Creative principle
Nature is only the emotional atmosphere; people doing small, comprehensible actions carry the motivational narrative. Avoid montage of interchangeable clouds or generic landscape b-roll. Every scene must reinforce the exact voiced clause.

## 5-act visual plan (timeline boundaries from genuine Vbee voice)
1. Hook — "Không ai vỗ tay..." Person alone on a winding mountain footpath; begin with visible movement (footsteps), wide silhouette against empty sky. Convey unrecognized effort.
2. Conflict — "Có những buổi tối..." A single person walking away at dusk / city lights from afar, cut to cloud-shadow and a quiet horizon; don't depict danger or self-harm.
3. Recovery — "Bạn tự học cách nuốt nước mắt... tự đứng dậy" Low angle shoes hiking an uphill dirt trail, visible progress across 2-3 beats; return to a new light beam through clouds as path opens.
4. Payoff — "Bạn đã trưởng thành rất nhiều..." Hiker reaches a viewpoint and remains standing as the clouds clear; a moving subject earns this emotional shift.
5. CTA — "thả tim và đăng ký kênh nhé." Serene sunrise with person in distant foreground; keep the audible and onscreen original CTA.

## Editing rules
- Alternate **human action (55–65%)** and **nature/sky scenery (35–45%)**. Do not lock to an arbitrary exact ratio if authentic scene footage is shorter/longer than the narration.
- Each source clip must be motion footage with verifiable source page, licensing and creator. Distinct locations, different framing and natural continuity; avoid same skyline repeated.
- Respect fixed Vbee Ngọc Huyền MP3 identity and word-for-word script. Place subtitles below CRT on safe area with authentic Be Vietnam Pro ExtraBold; 9:16 at 30 fps.
- Match cut movement direction where possible and keep CRT rounded matte, same geometry for frame and footage, from the V3 fix.
- Trim voice leading/trailing only as measured, gap ~0.12–0.4s; flag >0.65s unless explicit deliberate pause.
- Test full MP4 decode, 1–2fps and transition boundary images, sound, mobile reading, and manual visual beat-to-voice match before calling FINAL.
