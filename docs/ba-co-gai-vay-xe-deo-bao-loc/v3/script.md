## Direction
- 0–2s: immediately show the three white-clad women; 2–4s cut to driver's genuine reaction; 4–6s obscure their faces and introduce the mystery.
- 6–30s: legend background, rainy aftermath, shrine, recurring roadside sighting. Each clue is dramatized rather than asserted as historical proof.
- 30–48s: backpacks → braking → physically visible disappearance with matching before/after plates.
- 48–56s: rear-view mirror twist followed by caretaker's reported denial; mirror event is ORIGINAL FICTIONAL DRAMATIZATION.
- 56–60s: ONE story-specific spoken CTA containing like and follow, no extra title slate.
