# Production brief — Ba Cô Gái Vẫy Xe Trên Đèo Bảo Lộc

- Slug: `ba-co-gai-vay-xe-deo-bao-loc`
- Format: YouTube Shorts, vertical 9:16, target 60 seconds maximum, 30 fps.
- Genre: Vietnamese folklore / supernatural mystery; non-graphic psychological horror.
- Audience question: If three young women waved for a ride on a foggy mountain pass at night, would you stop?
- Viewer emotion: curiosity → unease → dread → lingering uncertainty.
- Visual language: cinematic noir, cold blue-black fog, wet asphalt, tungsten shrine candles, restrained amber and crimson accents.
- Transitions: gentle 0.35–0.55s fades/crossfades; smooth 2.5D camera motion. No shake, glitch, strobe or gore.
- Audio: genuine Vbee Batch narration after script lock; low ambient suspense, restrained road/wind/rain SFX; narration remains intelligible.

## Research boundary

The story is a circulating legend associated with Miếu Ba Cô on Đèo Bảo Lộc. The 2013 Bảo vệ Pháp luật article reports interviews with the shrine caretaker and local cultural representative; their accounts do not establish supernatural sightings as fact and include a denial that spirits regularly appear. Treat names, roadside sightings, backpacks/books and the rescue account as versions of oral retelling, not independently verified events. The film must say “chuyện kể”, “theo lời đồn”, or “một số tài xế kể”, and must not present paranormal claims as verified history.

Primary research reference:
- Bảo vệ Pháp luật, “Sự thật lời đồn 3 cô tóc dài, đêm đêm vẫy xe giữa đèo Bảo Lộc” (2013): https://baovephapluat.vn/phap-luat-ban-doc/bao-chi-cong-dan/su-that-loi-don-3-co-toc-dai-dem-dem-vay-xe-giua-deo-bao-loc-46667.html

## Creative hooks considered

1. Visual mystery: “Đêm nào cũng có ba cô gái đứng vẫy xe trên đèo Bảo Lộc…”
2. Driver POV: “Một tài xế nhìn thấy ba cô gái áo trắng giữa màn sương. Nhưng khi xe đến gần…”
3. Challenge / story question: “Nếu gặp ba cô gái này giữa đèo lúc nửa đêm, bạn có dừng xe?”

Selected: Hook 3 as the opening challenge integrated with the visual anomaly; hook must show the three figures in the first two seconds and the driver's reaction by second four.

## Character continuity

- Loan: young Vietnamese woman, long black hair, white áo dài, black backpack.
- Hòa: young Vietnamese woman, shoulder-length black hair, white áo dài, brown backpack.
- Thảo: young Vietnamese woman, low-tied black hair, white áo dài, dark blue backpack.
- Driver: Vietnamese man, early-to-mid 40s, dark jacket, cap, tired face, hands on steering wheel.
- Shrine caretaker: elderly Vietnamese woman, gray-black hair tied back, plain dark blouse.
- Ghost appearances retain the same three faces and white outfits as the memory/flashback shots. No unmotivated wardrobe or face changes.
