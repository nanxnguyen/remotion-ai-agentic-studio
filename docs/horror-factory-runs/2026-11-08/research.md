# Horror Factory research — 2026-11-08 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh)

## Candidate research

### 1) Ma Trành — selected
- **Classification:** folklore / literary legend, not a verified paranormal event.
- A 2022 Giáo dục Thủ đô article describes the legend that the spirit of a person killed and eaten by a tiger is compelled to lure another person to take its place. The article locates the legend in the Thanh–Nghệ–Tĩnh cultural context and discusses its later literary adaptations.
- Source: https://giaoducthudo.giaoducthoidai.vn/giai-ma-su-tich-tho-than-ho-va-chuyen-ma-tranh-4717.html
- Supplementary literary context: https://vanhoahoc.edu.vn/nghien-cuu/van-hoa-viet-nam/van-hoa-to-chuc-doi-song-ca-nhan/1885-tran-minh-thuong-ma-quy-trong-van-hoc-viet-nam.html
- Production guardrail: do not imply a real tiger attack, real missing person, or supernatural proof. The backward footprints and voice-copying mechanism in this film are original fictional devices, not claims about traditional belief.

### 2) Ma Lai — researched, not selected
- **Classification:** folk narratives and belief motifs documented in ethnographic/literary research, not evidence that such beings exist.
- Phan Xuân Viện's 2017 comparative study discusses 17 Ma Lai narratives among several Trường Sơn–Tây Nguyên communities and explicitly notes that accusations of being Ma Lai could stigmatize and harm real people.
- Source: https://khoavanhoc-ngonngu.edu.vn/nghien-cuu/van-hoc-dan-gian/6784-motif-trong-truy%E1%BB%87n-ma-lai-c%E1%BB%A7a-c%C3%A1c-d%C3%A2n-t%E1%BB%99c-thi%E1%BB%83u-s%E1%BB%91-%E1%BB%9F-tr%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Dng-s%C6%A1n-t%C3%A2y-nguy%C3%AAn-m%E1%BB%99t-v%C3%A0i-n%C3%A9t-so-s%C3%A1nh.html
- Production guardrail: not selected, partly to avoid turning a living ethnic community or a stigmatized accusation into a horror villain.

### 3) Bóng đè — researched, not selected
- **Classification:** a sleep-related experience that folk explanations may describe as a ghost pressing on a sleeper; not evidence of a supernatural event.
- A Cà Mau police article quotes a physician explaining sleep paralysis as a temporary mismatch between awareness and motor activity around waking.
- Source: https://congan.camau.gov.vn/ch11/243-Bong-de-nghet-tho-te-dai-Vi-sao-co-hien-tuong-nay.html
- Production guardrail: if used in a future story, distinguish the cultural interpretation from the medical explanation.
