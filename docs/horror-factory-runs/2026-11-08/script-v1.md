## Premise and story question

At an abandoned forest pass, Sơn's radio answers him in the voice of his missing father. A cassette warns him not to answer familiar voices and to inspect the footprints. He discovers that the figure is walking backwards to lure him deeper into the forest. Sơn escapes by following the chalk marks he placed on the way in, but the spirit leaves with a recording of Sơn's own voice.

**Question:** If a voice you love calls from the dark, would you answer?

## Hook options

1. **Selected — mystery + emotional contradiction:** Sơn transmits “Sơn đây, có ai nghe không?”; the radio replies in his missing father's voice: “Sơn, quay lại!”
2. **Visual surprise:** the footprints in the mud point toward Sơn while the figure walks away.
3. **Direct challenge:** “Đừng trả lời tiếng gọi ở Dốc Cọp.”

The selected hook shows the impossible radio response in the first two seconds. Opening challenge CTA appears as mobile-safe on-screen text: **BẠN DÁM NGHE HẾT?**

## Character and continuity bible

- **Sơn:** Vietnamese man, 27; short black hair; olive-green forest-ranger jacket with one reflective strip; dark trousers; black boots; canvas backpack. Same face, hair, jacket, and backpack throughout.
- **The caller / Ma Trành figure:** human silhouette in a faded old ranger cap and dark rain-soaked uniform; face never shown. Its signature action is stepping backwards while facing Sơn.
- **Environment:** fictional Dốc Cọp forest pass at night after rain; abandoned ranger hut, wet mud, bamboo and tall trees. No real address or missing-person claim.
- **Props:** handheld radio, cassette recorder in a dented metal tin, flashlight, white chalk. Sơn visibly marks trees with chalk before entering; those marks are his route back.
- **Visual language:** cinematic noir, blue-black forest, amber flashlight beam, restrained red radio LED, wet surfaces, restrained grain. No gore and no realistic tiger attack.
- **Sound:** original radio static, rain, low forest ambience and a restrained low-frequency threat cue. No unlicensed music or stock sound effects.

## Causal rules and payoff

1. The sourced folklore motif is that a Ma Trành spirit must lure another person to take its place.
2. For this fictional adaptation only, the caller imitates a familiar voice and walks backwards while luring its target. These details are original story devices, not traditional facts.
3. The father's tape establishes the response rule before the threat appears: do not answer; inspect the mud.
4. Sơn marks his own route with chalk before entering, so the escape action is prepared before the climax.
5. Sơn speaks into the radio in the opening. The final replay of that exact phrase proves the spirit has copied his voice; it does not claim Sơn was actually replaced.

## Description draft

Sơn trở lại dốc Cọp để tìm cuộn băng cuối cùng của người cha mất tích. Nhưng bộ đàm lại gọi anh bằng chính giọng của người đã biến mất.

Truyền thuyết Ma Trành kể về linh hồn người bị hổ ăn phải dụ người khác thế chỗ. **Đây là câu chuyện hư cấu lấy cảm hứng từ mô-típ dân gian**; chi tiết dấu chân đi lùi, bộ đàm bắt chước giọng nói và nhân vật Sơn đều do tác giả sáng tác, không phải sự kiện có thật.

Nếu nghe người thân gọi giữa rừng, bạn có trả lời không? Thả tim, bình luận và đăng ký Hẻm Chuyện Lạ để đón truyện tiếp theo.
