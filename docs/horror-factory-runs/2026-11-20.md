## Research — three Vietnamese supernatural-literature directions

All three are tales in the Vietnamese folklore collection *Lĩnh Nam chích quái*. They are **literary folklore/legend, not verified paranormal events**. NXB Khoa học Xã hội's 2026 interview with Hán-Nôm scholar PGS.TS Nguyễn Thị Oanh describes the collection as a body of Vietnamese folk legends and tales compiled around the late Trần period, and explains the complex manuscript transmission history: https://nxbkhxh.vass.gov.vn/hoat-dong-xuat-ban/nhung-truyen-thuyet-it-biet-ve-au-co-va-lac-long-quan-558455

1. **Hồ Tinh — cáo chín đuôi ở vùng Hồ Tây.** The literary account describes a white nine-tailed fox that disguises itself as a white-clothed person, lures people into a cave, and is defeated when the water-palace forces flood the cave; the sunken site becomes the Fox Corpse Pool/Hồ Tây in the story. Primary translated text: https://thuvien.vietnamthuquan.eu/TacPham/linh-nam-chich-quai-16477/chuong-4 . Textual/contextual analysis and explicit warning that it is absent from official histories: https://vietmyth.vn/entries/ho-tinh/
2. **Mộc Tinh / Xương Cuồng — cây Chiên Đàn ở Phong Châu.** The tale says the ancient tree withers and becomes a man-eating spirit; Kinh Dương Vương subdues but does not destroy it; people make a yearly human offering on the 30th day of the twelfth lunar month; a later magician lures the spirit to watch a performance and kills it. Primary translated text: https://thuvien.vietnamthuquan.eu/TacPham/linh-nam-chich-quai-16477/chuong-5 . Textual variants and caution against adding unsupported physical descriptions: https://vietmyth.shop/entries/moc-tinh/
3. **Ngư Tinh — quái ngư ở Đông Hải.** The creature lures immortals away from a rock-cut channel by disguising itself as a white rooster; Long Quân later tricks it with red-hot iron and the story explains several place names through its remains. Primary translated text: https://thuvien.vietnamthuquan.eu/TacPham/linh-nam-chich-quai-16477/chuong-3 . A second retelling of the same literary source: https://mythandmore.home.blog/2021/01/17/linh-nam-chich-quai-ngu-tinh/
