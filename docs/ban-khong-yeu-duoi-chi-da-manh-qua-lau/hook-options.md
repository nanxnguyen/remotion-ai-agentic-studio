Phase 2a hook engineering, per `.claude/skills/ray-pineline-worflow-agent/references/hook-engineering.md`.
Sources: `research.md` "Five hook possibilities" #1–#5 (primary source = the user's own account) and the owner's exact wording in `brief.md`.

Anti-clickbait rule applied to every candidate: no statistic, no shock claim, no withheld answer, nothing on screen the spoken line does not pay for. Every second quoted below is the same planning timeline as `script.md` §1 (142 syl, 3.9 syl/s, 13.05 s of motivated silence, total 49.46 s).

## H1 — Direct address · `Mọi người thường tìm đến bạn mỗi khi có chuyện buồn.` (research #1) → **CHOSEN** → `s1`

**1. Literal first frame (0–1 s) and safe-zone text.**
Frame 0 **is** the cover — already composed, no fade-in, no logo, no title animation: a 9:16 night-street mid-shot. **One person (the hero of the whole video) holds a black umbrella over someone smaller beside her.** Rain beads on the rim and drips off the lower edge onto wet pavement; **her own collar and shoulders are dry** — the frame states the thesis before a word is read. One warm street lamp behind; deep navy + soft grey everywhere else.
Safe-zone text (upper third, nothing in the bottom 210 px): **`Mọi người tìm bạn khi buồn.`** — 6 words, 26 characters, one line, readable sound-off in about 1.2 s at phone size; `bạn` in amber.

**2. Spoken 0–3 s line, syllables, sound-off.**
Spoken: `Mọi người thường tìm đến bạn mỗi khi có chuyện buồn.` = **11 space-separated syllables**. Clip `s1` runs **0.00–2.82 s**, so the hook is complete at **2.82 s** at the planning rate and still inside the window at the band edges (**2.62 s** at 4.2 syl/s, **2.97 s** at 3.7 syl/s).
Sound-off: **yes.** The image says "she is the shelter, they are dry, she is dry too"; the headline says "everyone comes to you when they are sad". Neither needs the audio.

**3. Tension / promise.**
Tension: the viewer is being *named*, not warned. Promise (truthful, no exaggeration): *I am going to describe a role you never chose and never asked for.* No number, no promise of a cure, no mystery — nothing the video does not pay for.

**4. First payoff before second 8, and where the ending resolves it.**
First concrete proof: **`s2`** — the recognition `Bạn biết cách an ủi.` lands at **4.45 s** (4.16 s at the fast rate, 4.67 s at the slow rate). The full payoff sentence `…thấy ổn hơn.` completes at **7.79 s** at the planning rate; in the slowest case only the *end* of the triad slips past 8 s (8.19 s) and the payoff itself is already delivered by 4.67 s. `a1` at 8.04 s then makes it visible — *others' shoulders sink, you stand outside*.
Resolution: the hook's promise is answered by the last two shots — `s10` asks the viewer the question nobody ever asked them (41.41–47.06 s) and the **END title card** carries the owner's own title line, silent, for 2.40 s (47.06–49.46 s). The umbrella opened in frame 0 is furled and closed on her lap in `s7`, **tilted away from her in `a4`**, and tilted **back over her in `s10`** — the visual promise resolves with the spoken one.

**5. Anti-clickbait risk and rejection of weaker variants.**
Risk: **lowest of the four** — no superlative, no statistic, no implication that the video will fix the viewer, no mystery. The only risk is flatness, and the cover image (dry shoulders against a soaked street) is built to defeat it.

## H2 — In-media-res · `Bạn muốn nhắn: 'Hôm nay mình mệt quá.'` (research #4)

**1. First frame / safe-zone text.** Macro of a phone screen lighting a dark face; the chat box is empty, the cursor blinking; headline `Mở app rồi lại đóng.` (5 words). Beautiful image — and, as frame 0, a stranger's private phone. Nobody in frame is doing anything the viewer can name.
**2. Spoken 0–3 s.** 8 syllables ≈ 2.05 s at the planning rate. Sound-off: **no** — the shot shows a phone, which could be anything; the word *muốn* (wants to) is the whole hook and it is audio-only.
**3. Tension / promise.** The withheld message, but a thin promise: it tells the viewer a feeling without telling them *why they should care*, and it opens at the most exposed moment of a scene they have not entered.
**4. Payoff.** Nothing concrete before second 8 (the line is the payoff itself, spent in its first 2 s); the middle has nowhere to go, and the reversal that explains it is 25 s away — the retention drop lands exactly where the value should have been.
**5. Risk / verdict.** Highest clickbait-shaped, lowest payoff-shaped. **Rejected as the opener; kept as `s4` (18.33–20.74 s), where it works because the viewer already owns the role — and where it is this video's re-hook.**

## H3 — Title line as reframing · `Bạn không yếu đuối, bạn chỉ đã mạnh mẽ quá lâu.` (research #5)

**1. First frame / safe-zone text.** The title itself on screen over a blurred portrait; no event, no action.
**2. Spoken 0–3 s.** 11 syllables ≈ 2.82 s at the planning rate. Sound-off: readable, but it reads as a **title card** — the exact blocker `hook-engineering.md` lists ("title-only fade", "title animation occupying first second").
**3. Tension / promise.** It is a **conclusion** ("you are not weak — you have been strong too long"). Conclusions make poor openers: there is nothing left to find out.
**4. Payoff.** Nothing to promise before second 8, because the payoff was already said in second one; the middle re-explains a line the viewer has already read.
**5. Risk / verdict.** No clickbait, but a **spoiler**: it hands the viewer the verdict before earning it. **Rejected as the opener; kept as the on-screen END title card (47.06–49.46 s, silent)**, which is exactly where `research.md` "Selected hook" places it ("the title line as closing re-hook/resolution"). It is on-screen only — the owner's ten spoken lines remain exactly ten.

## H4 — Contrast one-liner · `Bạn biết cách an ủi. Nhưng có một tối, chính bạn lại ngồi rất lâu trước màn hình điện thoại.` (research #2)

**1. First frame / safe-zone text.** Split frame: left, a hand on someone's shoulder; right, the same hand alone with a glowing phone. Headline `An ủi cả người. Ai an ủi mình?` — 7 words, over the 6-word cap, and it has to be cut.
**2. Spoken 0–3 s.** `Bạn biết cách an ủi.` = 5 syllables ≈ 1.28 s — the setup alone; the turn does not arrive until second 11, so the promised contrast is not paid inside the hook window.
**3. Tension / promise.** Strong truth, weak vantage point: "you know how to comfort" only lands if the viewer has already accepted the role.
**4. Payoff.** The turn arrives at 11.30 s — far too late to function as a hook payoff, and frame 0 has no anchor ("who is this person?").
**5. Risk / verdict.** Low clickbait, but a **re-hook used as an opener**: it spends the strongest contrast in the packet on seconds 3–11 and leaves the 15–20 s slot with nothing. **Rejected as the opener; the same contrast is preserved as `s2` → `a1` → `s3` (3.17–15.60 s), with the turn itself as highlight beat 1.**

## Decision

| Candidate | 0–1 s subject | Hook clear by 3 s | First payoff < 8 s | Sound-off | Payoff resolved | Clickbait risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** direct address | action: holds the umbrella, others dry, she is dry too | **yes, 2.82 s** (2.62–2.97 s across the rate band) | **yes, 4.45 s** (`s2`), triad complete 7.79 s | yes | `s10` + END title card + hold | low | **CHOSEN** → `s1` |
| H2 in-media-res | unnamed phone in the dark | yes, ~2.05 s | **no** | **no** | — | high (climax-first) | rejected → `s4`, the re-hook |
| H3 title line | no event | yes at the planning rate, but it is a **title card** | **no** | yes, as a title card | — | spoiler | rejected → END title card (silent) |
| H4 contrast | hand on a shoulder / same hand alone | partial | **no (11.30 s)** | partial | — | low | rejected → `s2`→`a1`→`s3` |

`H1` wins on the two literal tests the hook contract names first — an observable subject action inside the first second, and a truthful promise that is *paid* before second 8 — and because it is the only candidate whose ending keeps the same promise intact: the video opens by naming the role and closes by asking the question that role never receives.

**Named competing candidate for the reviewer to weigh:** `H2`. It is the strongest single image available in this story (a glowing phone in a dark room is the most scroll-stopping frame), and a reviewer may reasonably prefer it. The reason `H1` is better here: `H2` spends the video's most exposed line inside its own first two seconds with no context to be exposed against, delivers no proof before second 8, and cannot be understood with the sound off — three of the six selection criteria, against two for `H1` (`H2` wins only on raw image strength).

**Coverage check.** Hook complete 2.82 s · first proof 4.45 s · payoff sentence 7.79 s · **re-hook `s4` at 18.33 s** (17.24–19.17 s across the rate band, inside the 15–20 s window at every rate) · highlights `s3` / `s9`+`a4` / `s10` · silent hold 47.06–49.46 s · total 49.46 s (46.86 s at 4.2 syl/s, 51.43 s at 3.7 syl/s — inside the owner's 45–55 s in every case).
