## H1 — Direct address · `Mọi người thường tìm đến bạn mỗi khi có chuyện buồn.` (research #1) → **CHOSEN** → `s1`

**1. Literal first frame (0–1 s) and safe-zone text.**
Canonical cover description — reproduced **word for word** from `script.md` §2.2 and `brief.md` §Hook; gate D proves it reads at phone size on `out/qa/<vid>-cover.png` with the audio muted:

> Frame 0 **is** the cover — already composed, no fade from black, no logo, no title animation. 9:16 night street, mid-shot. **She holds the black umbrella out over the person beside her — a child, about her shoulder height — and it is tilted a few degrees toward them, so the rim's shadow falls across them and not across her.** Her right shoulder and the outside of her sleeve are already dark with rain, a wet stripe from shoulder to elbow; **she has not noticed and does not adjust.** The child's shoulder under the rim is dry. The contrast is carried by value and sheen rather than by a caption: **a wet sheen and a darker tone down her right sleeve, matte and dry on the child's coat and on the shoulder under the rim; wet sheen on the pavement below.** Water beads on the rim and drips off the lower edge. **Across the street one lit apartment window glows warm — a warmth she is not standing under.** One cold-warm sodium street lamp behind; deep navy and soft grey everywhere else. Slow push-in 1.00→1.03.

Three contract tests on that frame:
- **An action is in progress**, not a held pose: water is actively beading and dripping off the rim (the strongest motion in this frame at phone size), and the umbrella is mid-tilt toward the child and already failing to cover her.
- **The thesis is visible at thumbnail scale, and only one figure is wet — she is.** One dark wet stripe down her right sleeve against the child's matte, dry shoulder under the rim, split by the rim's shadow, with wet sheen on the pavement. It must read as a tone and sheen difference, not as a caption (`F-14`). This is also the polarity `s9`/`img2` pay later: her sleeve is already wet in frame 0 and gets wetter; the child stays dry for the whole runtime.
- The second figure is stageable without interpretation (a child at shoulder height), which the previous frame's unstageable wording was not.

Safe-zone headline (upper third, nothing in the bottom 210 px): **`Ai che ô cho bạn?`** — 5 words, a question, and **not** a compression of the spoken line. It holds from frame 0 and **clears on the `s1`→`s2a` boundary** (`S-05`).

**2. Spoken 0–3 s line, syllables, sound-off.**
Spoken: `Mọi người thường tìm đến bạn mỗi khi có chuyện buồn.` = **11 space-separated syllables**. Clip `s1` runs **0.00–3.34 s**; the hook is complete at **2.62 / 2.82 / 2.97 s** at 4.2 / 3.9 / 3.7 syl/s — inside the ≤3 s window across the whole band.
Sound-off: **yes.** The frame says "she is sheltering a child and is getting wet doing it"; the headline asks who shelters her.

**3. The gap the viewer is holding at 2.82 s (`H-07`, `H-08`).**
One question, stated in one sentence: **who holds the umbrella over you?** It is raised twice and answered twice — by the headline and by the lit window across the street she is not standing under; and paid at `s10`, when the hand of the saved name whose call rang unanswered at `s6` tilts the same rim back over her shoulder, and again on the silent END card. The promise the video makes is therefore the promise it pays: **nobody asks the person who holds the umbrella.**

**4. First payoff before second 8, and where the ending resolves it.**
The first staged payoff is **`a1`** — `Vai người khác chùng xuống. Còn bạn thì đứng ngoài.` — and it is **one beat, named once, in all three documents**. It lands at **7.00 / 7.46 / 7.81 s** at 4.2 / 3.9 / 3.7 syl/s, i.e. inside the 3–8 s window **at every rate in the band** (`H-01`). Owner line #2 is split into `s2a` + `s2b`, two verbatim halves, with `a1` between them across a real 0.25 s gap; `s2a` is the setup the payoff lands on, not a payoff itself.
Resolution: `s10`, which runs **39.76–45.70 s** (last word at **43.90 s**), pays the cover's question, and the **END card** carries the owner's title line, silent, **45.70–48.90 s**. The umbrella tilted toward the child in frame 0 is furled in her lap at `s7`, **tilted away from her at `s9`**, and tilted **back over her at `s10`** — the visual promise resolves with the spoken one.

**5. Anti-clickbait risk and rejection of weaker variants.**
Risk: **lowest of the four.** No superlative, no statistic, no implication that the video will fix the viewer, no mystery: the cover asks a question the video answers on screen at `s10` and on the last frame.
Weaker variants are re-argued below, including one whose recorded rejection reason was wrong (`H-06`).

## H2 — In-media-res · `Bạn muốn nhắn: 'Hôm nay mình mệt quá.'` (research #4)

**1. First frame / safe-zone text.** Macro of a phone screen lighting a dark head; headline `Một tin nhắn chưa gửi` (5 words).
**2. Spoken 0–3 s.** 8 syllables ≈ 1.95 s. Sound-off: **in this build, yes** — `script.alt-2.md` puts the **entire message text** in the bubble at frame 0 with the send key visible and unpressed and that exact label, which answers the sound-off objection outright. **Round 1 rejected this candidate on a straw-man (an empty box with a blinking cursor); the objection is withdrawn (`H-09`).** What actually survives: 8 syllables is thin for a first line; the author of the message is unnamed in frame 0; and that variant's runtime drops under 45 s if its three add-on lines are cut.
**3. Tension / promise.** The withheld message, but a thin promise — a feeling without a reason to stay — and it opens at the most exposed moment of a scene the viewer has not entered.
**4. Payoff.** Nothing concrete before second 8; the line is the payoff itself, spent in its first two seconds, and the reversal that explains it is far away.
**5. Risk / verdict.** Still rejected as the opener — but now for what it is, not for what a weaker build of it would have been. **Kept as `s4` (19.02–21.72 s), where it works because the viewer already owns the role, and where it is this video's re-hook.**

## H3 — Title line as reframing · `Bạn không yếu đuối, bạn chỉ đã mạnh mẽ quá lâu.` (research #5)

**1. First frame / safe-zone text.** The title itself over a blurred portrait; no event, no action.
**2. Spoken 0–3 s.** 11 syllables ≈ 2.82 s — inside the window by arithmetic only, and only because the line is a verdict. Sound-off: readable, but it reads as a **title card** — the exact blocker `hook-engineering.md` lists ("title-only fade", "title animation occupying first second").
**3. Tension / promise.** It is a **conclusion**. Conclusions make poor openers: nothing is left to find out.
**4. Payoff.** Nothing to promise before second 8, because the payoff was already said in second one.
**5. Risk / verdict.** No clickbait, but a spoiler: it hands the viewer the verdict before earning it, and it leaves the END card nothing to say. **Rejected as the opener; kept as the silent END title card (45.70–48.90 s)**, exactly where `research.md` "Selected hook" places it. It is on-screen only — the owner's spoken lines remain exactly ten.

## H4 — Contrast one-liner · `Bạn biết cách an ủi.` + `Nhưng có một tối, chính bạn lại ngồi rất lâu trước màn hình điện thoại.` (research #2)

**Round 1 rejected this candidate on a false premise; that is corrected here (`H-06`).**

**1. First frame / safe-zone text.** Split frame: left, a hand on someone's shoulder; right, the same hand alone with a glowing phone. Headline `An ủi cả người. Ai an ủi mình?` — 7 words, over the 6-word cap and it would have to be cut.
**2. Spoken 0–3 s, on its own syllables.** `H4` is **20 syllables** (5 + 15). Measured on its own syllables, not on the primary's timeline:

| rate | `H4`'s full contrast completes |
|---:|---:|
| 4.2 syl/s | **4.76 s** |
| 3.9 syl/s | **5.13 s** |
| 3.7 syl/s | **5.41 s** |

**It does pay a full contrast before second 8, at every rate.** The round-1 rejection ("the turn does not arrive until second 11") reasoned from the primary's timeline rather than `H4`'s own 20 syllables, and is withdrawn.
**3. Tension / promise.** Its payoff is a real contrast — *you comfort everyone, but one night you sat alone* — and it is the video's thesis rather than its premise.
**4. Payoff.** 4.76–5.41 s: **earlier than the selected `H1` manages (7.00–7.81 s).**
**5. Risk / verdict — its real costs.**
1. **Its 1–3 s window is the flattest sentence in the packet.** `Bạn biết cách an ủi.` names nobody, opens no question and raises no gap; the whole curiosity contract would have to be carried by the frame alone.
2. **It spends the video's only strong hinge in the first five seconds.** The `Nhưng` turn is the hinge the entire second half hangs from; using it as the opener leaves the 15–20 s re-hook slot with nothing new to say, and the owner's line #3 would arrive already spent at 5 s instead of at 11.94 s.
3. Its 0–1 s frame would be a split comparison rather than a single scene, and the selected cover carries the thesis in one readable image at thumbnail scale.

**Decision: keep `H1`.** `H4` is genuinely stronger on the payoff test, and that advantage is recovered inside `H1` instead: `H1` + `H-01` puts a staged contrast (`a1`) at **7.00 / 7.46 / 7.81 s** — inside the window at every rate — while `H1` still spends the `Nhưng` hinge at 11.94 s and still gives the re-hook slot a new event. `H4`'s cost is structural, not arithmetic.
