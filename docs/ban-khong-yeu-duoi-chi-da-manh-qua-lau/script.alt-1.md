## 6 · Split keys and the silence map

Cloned voices cannot be told to pause with punctuation, so every deliberate silence is either a **split key** (a real gap between two clips) or a **timeline hold** (real silence between clips). A pause is never requested by punctuation alone: the natural phrase break inside `s1` and `s3a` is a by-product of the sentence, and every *dramatic* beat below is a real gap between two clips in the timeline.

| silence | where | how | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.30 s | inside s3, between `nói gì.` and `Biết làm sao…` | **split key** `s3a` / `s3b` | the triad is a list; the gap is the beat between items |
| 0.80 s | inside s9, between `…quen với việc` and `mình phải là người ổn.` | **split key** `s9a` / `s9b` | the admission must land *in* the silence after the setup |
| 1.60 s | after s10 `Bạn biết không…` | timeline hold | the breath before the closing question — the longest beat in the video |
| 1.30 s | after s7 `bạn lại thôi.` | timeline hold | the give-up lands here, before the reversal |
| 2.60 s | after the closing line | timeline hold | the required silent hold; BGM out, only the image |
| 0.20 s | before the first syllable | timeline lead-in | frame 0 and the cover headline are readable before the voice starts |

`s10` keeps the owner's own ellipsis inside the clip (verbatim). The **1.60 s** beat that carries the drama is a timeline hold after the clip, not the punctuation — a one-clause fragment has no honest internal split point, and splitting it would either drop the owner's `…` or create a one-token clip.

It is an owner line, so it costs no invented speech.

**Contrast pairs (≥1 required, 2 given).**
- **CP1 · shelter / soaked.** frame 0: the umbrella open over someone else, **her shoulders dry** → `s5`: the same umbrella tilted *away*, a wet patch spreading on her shoulder while the person beside her stays dry → `s11`: the handle tilted back so the umbrella covers her too.
