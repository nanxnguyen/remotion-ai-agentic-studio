## A · The first second (0–1 s) — judged separately

**The exact frame I judged** (`script.md:62`, `hook-options.md:14`): *"9:16 night street, mid-shot: she holds a black umbrella over someone smaller beside her; rain beads on the rim and drips off the lower edge onto wet pavement; her own collar and shoulders are dry. **What passes, literally.** Frame 0 *is* the cover, already composed — no fade from black, no logo, no title animation, no generic greeting (`hook-engineering.md:7`). There is a nameable subject (a person with an open umbrella) and a nameable object action (holding it). The headline is 6 words / 26 characters, upper third, complete at frame 0, so the hook survives with the sound off (`hook-engineering.md:8`). No statistic, no shock claim, no withheld answer (`hook-options.md:7`). A slow push is a real motion decision, not a static title card.

**What fails, and it is the whole point of frame 0.**

1. **The frame does not show what the documents say it shows.** `hook-options.md:14` claims *"the frame states the thesis before a word is read"* because "her own collar and shoulders are dry". But in the frame as described, **both people are dry** — she is holding the umbrella over the other person, which is the umbrella working exactly as designed. Dry shoulders while holding an umbrella is the *unremarkable default state*. Nothing on screen contradicts anything, so no thesis is visible. The dry-shoulder idea only becomes an image at `a4` (`script.md:74`), when the umbrella is tilted *away* and a wet patch spreads — i.e. the frame-0 detail is the same detail as the late payoff, seen in its inert form, 34 seconds before it means anything. 2. **There is no action in progress.** "She holds" is a completed, static pose. `hook-engineering.md:7` asks for "an observable subject, problem, consequence, or surprising result" and `hook-engineering.md:22` names *in-media-res action — begin inside a concrete event* as the `story` pattern. Nothing is *happening* to her or to the frame in second one; the only movement is a 3 % camera push. `script.alt-2.md:18` shows what the same brief can produce: "A thumb hovers a few millimetres above the keyboard" — a decision visibly in progress. That is a strictly stronger 0–1 s image, and it exists inside this video's own option set.
3. **"Someone smaller" is not a stageable instruction.** A design agent cannot tell whether that is a child, a shorter adult, or someone seated, and the entire read of the frame depends on there being a second, smaller person under the rim. At phone size, in one second, the viewer may resolve "an umbrella and a shoulder" and not "two people."

## F · Structure and re-hook

- **Situation → turn → ending: present and correctly ordered** (`a1` 8.04 s is the turn's setup, `s3` 11.30 s is the hinge, `s6`/`s7` 25.42–31.55 s is the revelation, `s9`→`a4` 33.92–41.41 s is the metaphor landing, `s10` 41.41–47.06 s is the climax). ✓
- **Is ~18 s right for this runtime? Yes.** `s4` starts at 18.34 s (recomputed 18.335) of a 49.46 s video = 37 % through. `hook-engineering.md:11` asks for "around 15–20 s"; the band is calibrated for 30–45 s runtimes, and for 49.46 s a re-hook at 37 % is better than 33–40 % — the chosen point is inside the window at **every rate in the band** (17.24 / 18.34 / 19.17 s), which I verified. ✓
- **Is it a new event or a repeat of the opening? New.** `s1` = everyone comes to you. `s4` = you typed four words and did not send them. Nothing is repeated; it opens a question (`why not?`) whose immediate answer (`a3`, 22.98 s, the letters deleted) lands 4.6 s later and whose real answer (`s6`/`s7`) lands at 25.42 s. ✓ This beat is correctly designed and I have no structural complaint about it — only about the run-in (ticket **S-06**).
- **Does the ending resolve the hook's promise? Structurally yes; emotionally no.** The umbrella returns over her shoulder (`s10`) and the title card lands silent (`script.md:76`), so the visual promise is paid by the same object that opened it — the best structural closure in the script. But the *spoken* promise is paid by an unnamed hand, and the promise the hook actually made (`hook-options.md:22`: "I will describe a role you never chose") is not the promise the ending pays ("you deserve to be asked"). Ticket **H-08**.
- **Is the takeaway retellable in one sentence? Yes.** *"Even the person who always holds the umbrella for others gets rained on too — and deserves someone to ask how they're doing."* That survives a friend telling it at a dinner party. ✓ (Not my scored item; recorded because hook clarity depends on it.)
- **The street return is unbridged.** Frame 0 is a night street; `s2`–`s8` are a kitchen doorway and a bedroom; `s9` (33.92 s) is *"Back on the street in the rain"* with no time or place marker, and the script never states that it is the **same** black umbrella that was furled in her lap at `s7`. The emotional climax of the video therefore arrives with no location logic. Ticket **S-03**.

**Item 9 (profile `story` content test): 7.5 / 10.** Hero requirement ✓ — one consistent "her" with genuinely good prop continuity (the umbrella, the glass, the phone, the furled-in-lap umbrella); situation ✓ earned and staged; turn ✓ earned and corrected in the best beat of the video (the unanswered call); captions ✓ (word-for-word from `voice.proposed.json`, ≤2 lines, upper third for the cover, bottom edge clear of the 210 px zone); **ending ✗** — the one clause of the `story` content test that fails, for the reason in E5.
