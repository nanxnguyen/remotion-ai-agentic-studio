# Chiếc bát gọi tên — Script revision 3

Classification: FICTION_INSPIRED_BY_FOLKLORE. All people, incidents and supernatural rules below are invented. Inspiration is the broad Vietnamese folk motif of wandering spirits; no claim of a genuine ritual, witness or real haunting. Target: 55–60s including both CTAs; timings are editorial estimates until real Vbee measurement. Intended audience: adults, no gore.

Character bible: Lâm, Vietnamese man 28, short black hair, indigo long-sleeved shirt. Mother's portrait wears a muted plum áo bà ba. One white ceramic bowl with a cobalt chipped rim, one red cord, one handwritten note. Rainy old house, teal night, warm amber oil lamp. No real private location or identifiable victim.

## Storyboard and exact narration

1. CTA INTRO, estimate 0–3s. Macro bowl rises a few millimetres against taut red cord; a thin irregular shadow taps beneath the porcelain. Headline: AI Ở BÊN DƯỚI? Voice: “Bạn dám nhấc chiếc bát đang gõ?”
2. 3–7s. Lâm unpacks inherited house; mother's dated memorial portrait and bowl share frame; beside them a yellowed photo shows her hand stopping six-year-old Lâm beside this same cobalt-chipped bowl. Voice: “Mẹ mất ba năm. Lâm tìm thấy bát úp, buộc dây đỏ.”
3. 7–11s. He reads note tied to cord; leaves bowl inverted. A thin red cord crosses its crown and anchors through two eyelets in a wooden tray; the loose friction knot is visible. The note is visibly folded twice. Voice: “Giấy mẹ dặn: Mẹ đã đáp nó. Con đừng đáp. Đừng mở.”
4. 11–15s. Rainy door shakes; bowl shadow lengthens toward it, visible but not explained. Voice: “Nửa đêm, ngoài cửa vang giọng mẹ: Lâm ơi!”
5. 15–19s. Lâm takes one step toward door; sees portrait; lips part to call “mẹ”, then he stops himself, covers his mouth and deliberately fastens the bolt. Voice: “Cậu nhìn di ảnh, khóa chặt cửa.”
6. 19–23s. Voice relocates to bowl; an indentation presses from underneath. Voice: “Tiếng mẹ chuyển xuống bát. Nút dây đang tuột.”
7. 23–28s. The knot slips, bowl rim lifts and black vapour flows toward his fingers. The slack cord end slides toward the lamp. Lâm catches it with a poker, pins the bowl to its tray and pulls the cord taut; vapour visibly retreats under the rim. This before/action/result is mandatory actual motion, never a single still zoom. Voice: “Lâm kéo dây khỏi lửa, ghì bát xuống.”
8. 28–33s. Under-bowl voice changes to a six-year-old boy’s voice; he stares, mouth visibly closed. Voice: “Bên dưới bật lên giọng trẻ con: Mẹ ơi, con đây!”
9. 33–39s. Cord snags note; it unfolds a previously hidden second flap. Voice: “Mảnh giấy mở nếp gấp: Nó giữ giọng người từng đáp.”
10. 39–45s. Clearly signalled memory insert of his childhood in same house, responding “Con đây” to the bowl’s projected “Con ơi”, then mother visibly pulling him away; return to his eyes. Voice: “Lâm nhớ lúc sáu tuổi. Bát gọi: Con ơi. Cậu đáp: Con đây.”
11. 45–52s. He reties cord rather than lifting bowl. The projected sound remains below the bowl. The red knot tightens and bowl stops lifting; the same remembered six-year-old voice remains audibly trapped below, with no claim of a child body inside. Voice: “Cậu thắt lại nút. Bát hết nhấc. Tiếng của cậu năm sáu tuổi vẫn gọi mẹ.”
12. CTA OUTRO, 52–57s estimate, after full reveal. Locked bowl, Lâm withdrawn beside portrait, the red-cord shadow completes one tap; no new plot event after CTA. Headline: GIỌNG AI ĐANG GỌI? Voice: “Bạn có đáp không? Thả tim, đăng ký Hẻm Chuyện Lạ.”

## Continuity and action contract
The entity stays confined by an inverted cord-bound bowl. The voice at door is projected, with its source planted as the bowl shadow in shot 4. It can borrow voices from prior responders: mother (her note explicitly says she answered it) and six-year-old Lâm, not adult Lâm or an unexplained stranger. Lâm never deliberately lifts the bowl or unties the cord; the entity can raise its rim slightly until he retightens the restraint. The cord anchors to the wooden tray at two eyelets, not simply around the bowl. The concealed flap of the note is visible folded in shot 3, opened only by physical cord action in shot 9. Main character chooses restraint twice, then repairs the physical seal: the entity can project stored voices but cannot lift the bowl when the cord is tight. The cord never promises to silence it. Childhood flashback is marked by warm lighting, smaller character and separate memory transition, never presented as current action.
