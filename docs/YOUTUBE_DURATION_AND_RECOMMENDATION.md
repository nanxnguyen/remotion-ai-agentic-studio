# YouTube Video Duration & Recommendation Guidelines

This note summarizes how YouTube's recommendation algorithm treats video duration for both **Long-Form** and **Shorts**, and how to choose target runtimes for channels in this repository.

## Core Principle: No "Magic Runtime"

YouTube's algorithm does **not** boost a video merely because it hits a specific minute mark (such as 8, 15, or 30 minutes). Instead, recommendations are driven by:

1. **Viewer Satisfaction** (surveys, engagement, and lack of early drop-off).
2. **Audience Retention** (percentage of the video watched).
3. **Total Watch Time & Session Duration** (how many minutes a video contributes and whether it keeps viewers on YouTube).

Padding a 5-minute story to 15 minutes with filler lowers retention and hurts recommendations.

---

## 1. Long-Form Videos (16:9 Main Feed)

| Runtime Tier | Best Use Case | Algorithmic Trade-Offs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4–10 minutes** | Quick explainers, news commentary, concise reviews | Easier to hold **50%+ retention**, but lower watch time per view. |
| **8+ minutes** | Mid-roll ad eligibility | **Monetization threshold only** (enables mid-roll ads); not an algorithmic boost on its own. |
| **12–20 minutes** | Storytelling, video essays, structured deep dives | **Core sweet spot**: clears the 8-minute ad floor while keeping retention healthy and generating solid watch time per view. |
| **20–30+ minutes** | Long-form history, folklore horror, documentaries, podcasts | Highest **Watch Time** and **Session Duration** upside if pacing is strong and chapters are clear. High risk if viewers bounce in the first 1–2 minutes. |

### Practical Pacing & Hook Rules
- **First 30 seconds:** Front-load the core premise/hook immediately; avoid slow intros or logo bumpers.
- **Script pacing:** Natural narration averages ~150 words/minute (~750 words for 5 min, ~1,200 words for 8 min, ~1,500 words for 10 min). Cut dead air and repetition.
- **Channel strategy (YPP 4,000 watch-hour goal):**
  - Start new/growing storytelling series at **10–18 minutes** to balance retention and watch time.
  - Scale to **25–35+ minutes** (e.g., full historical arcs or *Dạ Hành Dị Tạp* folklore episodes) only when the script has enough turns/chapters to sustain retention.

## 2. YouTube Shorts (9:16 Ecosystem)

Shorts run on a separate recommendation system from long-form:

- **Technical limit:** Up to 3 minutes.
- **Algorithmic sweet spot:** **22–45 seconds** (most top-performing Shorts sit within **30–60 seconds**). Going past 60 seconds requires exceptionally tight pacing.
- **Key ranking signals:**
  - **Swipe-Away Rate (first 2–3 seconds):** If more than ~30% of viewers swipe away immediately, distribution drops sharply.
  - **Retention targets:** Aim for **~65%+** on clips under 30 seconds, and **~50%+** on clips between 30 and 60 seconds.
