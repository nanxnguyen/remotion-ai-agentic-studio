# Character bible — An

- Adult fictional Vietnamese protagonist; expressive white stick-figure skeleton, near-black environment, warm amber signal.
- **Shape lock:** head circle r=75 on 1080×1920 viewport, shoulders and body attached, stroke width 9–10px, consistent single screen anchor x≈435, y≈865 head and y≈1200 torso, feet at y≈1403.
- **Orientation:** 3/4 facing camera, gaze shifts by only a few pixels. Never mirror-flip without reason.
- **Five poses:** (1) waiting / phone hovering near wrist, (2) overworked / lowered shoulders, (3) listening / reaching toward another, (4) overwhelmed / motion slowing and one tear, (5) boundary / arm relaxed, candle gesture, smile restoring.
- **Physiology:** continuous breathing every ~96 frames with a 5px displacement. Face/arm transition is interpolated across shot boundary; no reset or teleport.
- **Props:** notification card, physical phone, cake, paper and candle are anchored in the right half. Phone glow becomes candle warmth. No gore, no photorealistic portraits, no third-party brand elements.
- **Emotional arc:** restrained hurt → sudden recognition → regained agency. Never make An look euphoric instantly after crying.
