## Audience and arc

Opening friction becomes a small, inspectable success rather than a list of transition features. No forced comedy. Callback: the same dot that jumped in s1 reaches the final checkpoint in s5.

## Voice-performance handoff

- `voice.proposed.json` remains the canonical ordered text. Joining each shot's phrase texts from `voice-performance.proposed.json` with one space must reproduce its canonical string byte for byte after UTF-8 loading; acting directions and emphasis metadata are never spoken or captioned.
- `voice-direction.md` defines the audience relationship, intent/emotion curve, emphasis, pace changes, explicit breath gaps and line ending for every shot. Phrase pauses are assembly gaps, not punctuation-dependent timing. Shot-tail and final holds remain timeline gaps outside the generated clip.
- Use one benchmark-selected VieNeu-TTS 3.8.3 reference across all five shots. The shortlist is `Anh-Khoi-Ke-Chuyen.mp3`, `Thanh-Long-Nang-Cao.mp3`, and `Ngoc-Huyen.mp3`; source previews stay read-only and local. No reference is selected until the common benchmark is measured.
- Only verified controls are proposed: local reference audio, candidate seed and `denoise: false`. Rate, pitch, volume, direct emotion/style controls, `atempo` and experimental inline emotion cues are disabled. A voice producer must report a limitation instead of silently ignoring an unsupported request.
- Initial generation uses one candidate per phrase. Regenerate only a failed phrase or keyword, up to three candidates for that phrase; every selected take still passes canonical-text/CER checks. Machine reports separate technical fidelity from naturalness/emotional credibility, which are labeled `not verified by listening` without an actual listening record.
