## Premise and story question

On the night of her first death anniversary, Linh rides home after her mother's text "Về ăn cơm nhé con". She does not know she died in the building fire a year ago. The elevator stops at a floor 13 that exists only for her: her own sealed home, where her mother waits at her altar.
**Story question (by 6 s):** why does a 12-floor building have a floor 13 tonight, and what is waiting there?

## Hook

Winning candidate **H1** (visual surprise). See [hook-options.md](hook-options.md) for H1–H5 with first frame, 0–3 s syllables, muted legibility, tension, payoff and clickbait risk. Frame 0 = cover: 12-button panel + display; display flips 12 → 13 at 1.5 s; amber "TẦNG 13" by 1.5 s.

## Structure

- **HOOK 0–6 s**: rule (12 floors) broken on screen at 1.5 s → doors open on 13; Linh looks up, phone shows Mẹ's text.
- **BODY 6–30 s**: doors won't close, cabin light dies (motive to step out) → identical corridor, every door sealed → seal date a year ago, at midnight → only her door 1205 ajar → TV already on, smell of incense, someone sitting back-turned.
- **CLIMAX 30–48 s, two stages**: (1) TV replays old news: this very 12-floor building burned at midnight, timestamp 00:12 on screen (no year), one young woman did not escape → victim photo → (real silence) → "Là Linh."; (2) the person turns: her mother with incense; altar with Linh's portrait and a full rice bowl.
- **ENDING 48–60 s**: mother whispers "hôm nay giỗ đầu con… con về ăn cơm với mẹ nhé" (callback to the 0–6 s text) while Linh's fingertips turn to ash → the rice bowl stays full, chopsticks wait; 0.8 s hold, fade to black.

## Narration rules applied

- Numbers spelled for Vbee: "mười hai", "mười ba", "mười hai không năm". Captions show digits.
- No fiction disclaimer in voice; "Truyện hư cấu" in the description. No real building name: the fictional news says "chung cư 12 tầng" only.
- Every narrated physical event has a planned visible before → action → result (V3→V4 gate): display flip, doors opening, finger presses with doors unmoved, cabin light dying, seals revealed, date traced, door swinging wider, hand pushing door, incense smoke in TV light, fire footage with timestamp 00:12, portrait sliding in, Linh stepping back, mother turning, tilt-up to portrait, mother whispering in the same two-shot as ash fingertips, untouched rice bowl.
- Twist silence is real: s9 "Ảnh nạn nhân hiện lên." and s10 "Là Linh." are separate clips with a 0.7 s timeline gap.
- Transitions: soft fades 0.3–0.6 s (s1→s2 is 0.3 s so Linh's face reads ≥1.2 s in the 2–4 s window); fade-to-black ending. No shake, glitch, flash, whip.
- Art route (NO_CARTOON_ART_V1): 16 photoreal cinematic frames generated with Gemini from [image-prompts.md](image-prompts.md), stored in `frames/p01..p16.png` (1536×2752). "2.5D" means camera push/pan, parallax crops, light flicker, smoke/ash particle overlays and crossfades on these photos; it is not independent human acting and is disclosed as limited motion. All in-frame text (display digits, seal time, phone message, TV lower thirds) is composited in Remotion, never generated.

## Timing method

162 spoken syllables. At Vbee storytelling pace (~4.0–4.5 syl/s at speed_rate 1.05) speech is ~35–40 s; the rest is designed silence (ellipsis pauses, the 0.7 s twist gap, 0.8 s final hold) and visual action time inside each slot. Slots are budgets: at Join every scene is re-timed to its measured clip + hold, so the final runtime may land between ~50 and 60 s (never above 60.000 s). A clip longer than its slot minus 0.08 s means a text rewrite and a new Gate A, never a time-stretch.
