## Cultural grounding (common knowledge, no external claim used on screen)

- Vietnamese urban viewers live in or visit *chung cư*; elevator displays, shoe racks outside doors, and paper seals (giấy niêm phong) with red stamps after an incident are familiar images.
- *Giỗ đầu* is counted on the **lunar calendar**, so it rarely falls on the same solar date; the film therefore shows no year and no solar-date arithmetic, only "một năm trước" and the family's own "hôm nay giỗ đầu".
- *Giỗ đầu* (first death anniversary): family lights incense at an altar with the deceased's portrait (di ảnh) and invites the soul home to eat ("về ăn cơm"). This is the emotional engine of the payoff.
- Many buildings avoid labelling floor 13; using a 12-floor building makes "13" impossible without needing that superstition explained.
