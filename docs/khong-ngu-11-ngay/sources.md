# Scientific Sources — 11 Days Without Sleep (`khong-ngu-11-ngay`)

1. **24 hours awake (~0.10% BAC equivalence):** Dawson & Reid (1997), *Nature* — 24 hours of sustained wakefulness produces cognitive psychomotor impairment equivalent to ~0.10% blood alcohol concentration.
2. **48 hours awake (Immune suppression):** Besedovsky et al. (2012), *Pflügers Archiv* — sleep deprivation impairs natural killer (NK) cell activity and elevates inflammatory cytokines.
3. **72 hours awake (Microsleeps):** Short 3–15 second involuntary episodes of sleep occurring while eyes remain open, causing severe attention lapses.
4. **96–120+ hours awake (Hallucinations & Paranoia):** Coren (1996) / Randy Gardner case logs — visual distortions, hypnagogic hallucinations, and acute paranoia.
5. **1964 Randy Gardner Experiment (264.4 hours / 11 days):** Documented by Dr. William Dement (Stanford) — stayed awake 264 hours 12 minutes, then slept 14 hours 40 minutes on recovery night.
