# Beat-sync decision — dung-bien-tuoi-30-thanh-ban-an

The selected BGM is the deterministic soft-story pad/arpeggio generated by `scripts/gen-bgm-soft.py`. It has a gentle 72 BPM source pulse, but this edit is not beat-synced: shot boundaries, the re-hook, the climax, and the final 2.4-second silence are governed by measured voice spans and intentional story holds in `script.md`.

No beat grid is consumed by the timeline, so `analysis/beat_data.json` and `grid_drift.json` are intentionally not produced. SFX cues are anchored to the start of their shot and to visible foley actions or post-line holds, never to interpolated absolute frames.
