# Film brief — Chiếc Lọ Đựng Ác Mộng

- **Story in one sentence:** A creature mistaken for a nightmare is secretly guarding a little boy's sleep, until the little boy learns to guard the exhausted creature in return.
- **Audience:** Vietnamese-speaking Shorts/TikTok family and emotional-animation viewers; age-appropriate 3D fantasy mystery.
- **Duration:** target 45.0s, 12 purpose-driven story beats. 9:16 1080×1920; 60fps only when underlying 3D character motion genuinely supports it.
- **Viewer question:** What is the monster stealing from Nam under the bed, and what happens when its nightmare jar breaks?
- **Primary emotion:** Fear → surprise → sympathy → terror → tender relief.
- **Two contrast images:** Claw nearing child (fear) versus the same claw shielding him (love); at the end, protective hands reverse (Nam hugs Mực).
- **One driving prop:** jar gathering smoke-nightmares; one safety motif: star-shaped bedside lamp whose warm light repels nightmare smoke.
- **Hook:** H1 in `hook-options.md`; direct claw visible on frame 0, mystery clear by 0–6s, first small answer at 6–10s.
- **Re-hook:** 17–20s crack in overfilled jar.
- **Climax:** 24–36s jar shatters and monster shields child; he sees it firsthand.
- **Payoff:** 36–45s Nam actively turns on lamp and hugs protector; warm music resolves.
- **Visual identity:** original stylized 3D, indigo fuzzy Mực (left ear nick, amber eyes), child Nam (blue pajamas with a star), one coherent room and stable prop/eyeline geometry. No existing studio character imitation.
- **Source claims:** fictional original tale; no factual external claims. This is not a rendered production.
