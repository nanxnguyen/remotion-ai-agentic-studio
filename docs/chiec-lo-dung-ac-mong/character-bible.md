# Character / location bible — Chiếc Lọ Đựng Ác Mộng

**Film style:** original family-safe stylized cinematic **3D animation**, not a lookalike of any copyrighted studio property. Tender, eerie, child-appropriate, visually consistent across all 12 microshots. Vertical 9:16, target 1080×1920, 60fps target if genuine intermediate animation can support it.

## Nam — boy, 7–8

- Short black straight hair, one cowlick over **left eyebrow**; round cheeks and eyebrows carrying his emotion.
- Starry **sky-blue pajamas** with a tiny white embroidered star over left chest, no shirt changes during night; barefoot.
- First 32s asleep, facial fear and brow tension; sees sacrifice in S10, wakes and chooses to act in S11; final tear and calm relieved smile. No random age/face changes.
- Physical height ~four times Mực's standing height. Reach bedside star-lamp switch from left edge of pillow when awake.

## Mực — small under-bed guardian

- Child-sized **indigo/navy fluffy creature**, round pear-shaped body (about 50 cm tall), golden/amber eyes, **small left-ear nick**, three short retractable claws on each hand, short legs; never a giant demon.
- A tiny canvas satchel carries the same stoppered **glass nightmare jar**. Gold eye glow fades at peak exhaustion and recovers through the boy's hug/light.
- Acting: alert nervous → careful gentle collection → fatigue → fear → protective stance → shock at receiving care → soft, genuine sleep.
- Movement: lopes on four paws exiting bed; bipedal at jar and shield shots, consistent foot/ear/tail and attached props. Not a still PNG zoom.

## Ác mộng / nightmare shadow

- Smoke-like pitch black with deep violet interior and only **two tiny red points** hinting at a face; NO gore, blood, sharp teeth or nightmare violence beyond looming shadow.
- Always **recoils from amber starlight**, planted visibly in S03 (weak light) and S08 (avoids light), paid off in S11 (lamp brightly switched on). Disperses safely into tiny stars; do not imply a supernatural explosion from the hug alone.
