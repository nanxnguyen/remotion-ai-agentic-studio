## Emotional structure

- **0–6s hook:** frightening claw instantly visible → hand intentionally avoids the boy; ask what the monster is stealing.
- **6–13s first payoff:** monster extracts nightmares; **nightmare physically recoils from even weak star-lamp light** (critical setup).
- **13–24s empathy + re-hook:** exhausting nightly task → 17s jar crack → futile repair.
- **24–36s climax:** large nightmare escapes; frightened tiny monster shields the boy, weakens; Nam actually SEES this sacrifice.
- **36–45s earned turn:** the awake boy chooses to turn on lamp (setup paid off), hugs protector, nightmare dissipates; caretaker cared for.
