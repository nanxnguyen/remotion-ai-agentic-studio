## Retention editing

- 0–2s: unusual camera image + immediate stakes; no logo intro.
- 2–12s: establish predictive-photo rule with broken-glass evidence.
- 12–30s: meaningful escalation through accident, faceless woman and locked room.
- 30–45s: shutter at 03:00, 13th-floor window and impossible double.
- 45–60s: reveal who is being replaced; earned ending and shutter SFX.
- Typography: Noto Sans Display Condensed Black, ivory/amber/dark crimson; one headline, one kicker and one subline per scene, designed for 9:16 mobile-safe area.
- Narration: **new genuine Vbee Batch TTS** generated from the approved rewritten timeline, not mismatched old audio.
- Motion: continuous slow dolly/push, 9 restrained cinematic transitions; avoid excessive strobing.

## Post-publication measurement

Track **Stayed to watch**, **Engaged views**, **Average view duration**, **Average percentage viewed**, and the **retention graph** after 24h and 7d. Test one creative variable at a time in future uploads. These are experiments, not guaranteed view multipliers.
