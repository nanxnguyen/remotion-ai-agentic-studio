# Creative brief — Bức Ảnh Duy Nhất Không Có Mẹ

- Production skill: **ray-emotional-stick-shorts** (specialization of ray-video-10-phut; does not supersede canonical pipeline gate rules)
- Audience: Vietnamese short-form viewers touched by family memories, invisible parental support, kindness and gratitude
- Emotional thesis: The person who kept every family memory is the person missing from nearly every photo
- One recurring object: **mother's phone camera** passes from mother to daughter in the final beat
- Hook target: first **5.3 seconds**, real action of Linh swiping the family album, then freezing
- Target: **44.2 sec**, **18 purposeful microshots** in nine dramatic pairs; 1080×1920 native 60 FPS
- Voice: nine actual Vbee Thanh Long Vietnamese utterances, measured for natural rhythm; original piano ducked under speech
- Color: ink #08090B, white #F5F5F4, gray #85878A, amber #EF9E69; no unnecessary decoration
- Absolute hard constraint: **NEVER publish to YouTube**. vidIQ media-library import after technical pass is not publication.

## Hook options considered
1. **Chosen — a specific anomaly:** “Linh lật album ảnh gia đình... hơn trăm bức ảnh, mà chẳng thấy mẹ.”
2. Curiosity: “Ai là người duy nhất chưa từng đứng trong ảnh gia đình?”
3. Personal regret: “Linh tìm mẹ trong ảnh... và nhận ra một điều khiến cô bật khóc.”

Hook #1 places a concrete visual search and an unresolved question in the first frame. Every later shot pays off a piece of that visual puzzle.

## Chosen emotional shape
Album absence → warm birthday → graduation → dinner → lonely repetition → unexpected reflection → kitchen realization → invitation → complete portrait.

## Known evaluation limits
Automated FFmpeg/visual-boundary measurements are **not** independent script gate, naturalness, acting or attention analytics. Human listening/viewing and canonical pipeline gates remain separate and are not invented.
