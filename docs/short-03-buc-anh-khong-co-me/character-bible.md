# Character bible and continuity locks

## Linh (daughter)
- Consistent dark **ponytail**, tiny **amber hair pin**, slim white line silhouette and young upright stance.
- At birthday memory use ~0.67 adult scale to represent young Linh; as an adult in graduation and present day ~0.92–1.0.
- Emotional performance: initial curious scrolling → joy in memories → surprised realization → tear in kitchen → reach for mother → natural smile in family portrait.
- Motivated hand poses: hold album, reach toward reflection, take mother's hand, finally handle photo camera.

## Mother
- Silver/grey low bun, **round spectacles**, modest cardigan with one amber button, slightly bent and gentle posture.
- Her hand **holds camera/phone** in birthday, graduation and dinner. No unnoticed outfit/hair swapping.
- Smile is a modest, quiet pride; no melodramatic cries or unnecessary movements.
- Reflection in graduation glass repeats the same bun, cardigan, camera and face, not a visually unrelated shadow.
- Kitchen scene: the same hands which took photos now clear bowls.

## Identity and blocking
- Mother: slate-grey outlines, warm amber detail; Linh: white contour and narrow amber hair pin.
- Distinct landmarks must persist in wide, insert and reflection.
- Daughter and mother are never drawn as visually identical figures in the same spatial location.
- Family group excluding mother repeats in birthday, graduation and dinner imagery; ending **adds mother centrally**.
- Visual transformation: mother's hand holding the camera → daughter's hand using the camera.
- Global deterministic frame clock; camera motion must not reset at each cut.

## Technical caveat
Drawn figures are frame-driven Python/Pillow 2-D animation reused as a production route, not proof of a canonical Remotion composition registration or full pipeline reviewer passes.
