## 3 · Hook engineering gate

### 3.1 First second, separately from the first three

**First second (frame 0).** The frame is the cover (`script.md:60`): eye level across a rice-shop counter at dusk; two identical white takeaway boxes on wet stainless steel, six inches apart; the right box has a red elastic band; steam still escapes the left lip; a thermal receipt is mid-print, `×2` already legible; the narrator's right hand is still open after laying a second pair of wooden chopsticks on the right box; the vendor's out-of-focus hand waits. The hook block is the only overlay text, four lines, lower third. The question is immediate and concrete: **why is one person buying two?** This is not a generic breakup image; the receipt and the second chopsticks are the specificity.

**First three seconds.** "Tôi vẫn mua hai phần ăn theo thói quen" is 9 syllables ≈ 2.09 s of speech, ending at ≈2.29 s absolute after the 0.20 s lead-in; the turn "Đến lúc tính tiền..." begins at ≈2.43 s after the 0.14 s gap. The full 19-syllable sentence takes 4.42 s, so the spoken twist *completes* after 3 s, but it *begins* inside 3 s. Sound-off, the full sentence is readable in ≈2.5 s of the 4.4 s shot, and the two boxes + `×2` carry the anomaly from frame 0. A cold viewer stops on the object and the text; a listener-only viewer gets the setup and the start of the turn inside 3 s. This is the best achievable with the owner's mandated line, and it is declared rather than hidden (`hook-options.md:30–33`). Hook score 9.0, not 10.

### 3.2 First payoff (3–8 s)

The printer finishes `×2` at 4.76 s; at 5.26 s the hard cut lands on the toothbrush holder — one dry brush, one wet — the same habit in a second object before 8 s (`hook-options.md:36–38`, `script.md:79`). This is a truthful first payoff: it repeats the behaviour, it does not introduce a new claim or a new character.

### 3.3 Ending resolves the promised tension

The promise is "my body is still shopping for two people." s5 reverses the same counter and the same receipt to `×1`; s6 opens the one box, eats it, and refuses the uplift: "Không thấy nhẹ người. Chỉ thấy no." (`script.md:83–84`). The tension is resolved by a visible act, not advice. There is no CTA, no moral, no healing arc.

### 3.4 Winner vs rejected candidates

H1 (39/40, `hook-options.md:46`) beats H2 "Chị bán hàng hỏi: Hai hộp hả em?" (34/40) and H3 "Có một thứ chia tay không dọn đi." (31/40). H2 is more immediately dramatic but invents vendor dialogue, and the brief says the vendor has no dialogue in the final script (`brief.md:242`). H3 is abstract and could belong to any breakup video. H1 is the owner's line, first person, and the image *is* the line. The chosen hook is the strongest of the three for this brief, not merely the mandated one.

### 3.5 What only rendering or publication can prove

Whether the `×2` is legible at phone size in the first frame; whether the four-line hook block fits at 76 px or must shrink to 64 px (`script.md:66`); whether a cold viewer actually stops (publication metric); whether the 1.80 s hold reads as a hold rather than a frozen export.
