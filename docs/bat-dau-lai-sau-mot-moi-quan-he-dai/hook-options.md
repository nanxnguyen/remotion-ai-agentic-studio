# Hook options — bat-dau-lai-sau-mot-moi-quan-he-dai

Read before writing: `.agents/skills/ray-pineline-worflow-agent/references/hook-engineering.md`.
Three substantially different mechanisms, not three rewordings. H1 is the owner's mandated line
(verbatim) and stays the default choice unless it is clearly unclear or unsafe.

Scoring axes (each /10):
`S` source support (owner material or a URL in `research.md`) · `O` sound-off comprehensibility
(frame 0 + on-screen text alone, no audio) · `F` first-frame visual potential at 9:16 / phone size ·
`P` payoff resolution (does the video actually answer what the hook promises, by the end?).

Research supplies five mechanisms (H1 confession, H2 surprise/contrast, H3 curiosity gap, H4
concrete object, H5 personal stake). The three candidates below are **instantiations** of H1, H2 and
H3 for this film's props: H1 keeps the owner's mandated wording; H2 and H3 are re-worded so the
first frame is shootable in this video's world instead of a generic breakup image. H4 was not
instantiated because its image collapses into H1's two boxes; H5 was not instantiated because it
pre-reveals the ending (`hook-engineering.md`: no hook unrelated to the payoff).

---

## H1 — "Tôi vẫn mua hai phần ăn theo thói quen." (specific personal stake · **selected**)

- **First frame (0–1 s):** eye-level wide of a rice-shop counter at dusk. Two takeaway boxes
  on wet stainless steel, one with a red elastic band, steam rising from the just-packed rice. The
  receipt printer is mid-print, a narrow strip curling up. Hook text sits in the lower third, four
  short lines, the frame's only text.
- **On-screen text (sound off):**
  `Tôi vẫn mua hai phần ăn` / `theo thói quen.` / `Đến lúc tính tiền mới nhớ` / `giờ chỉ còn mình.`
  (verbatim = the spoken line; the hook shot's text block replaces the caption, so nothing is said twice).
- **Spoken 0–3 s:** the mandated two sentences, 19 syllables, ≈4.4 s to finish at 4.3 syl/s; the
  first clause ("Tôi vẫn mua hai phần ăn theo thói quen") closes at ≈2.3 s and already states the
  behaviour. Comprehension does not depend on hearing the twist: the two boxes plus the printed
  `×2` carry it from frame 0, and the full sentence is readable in ≈2.5 s of the 4.4 s shot.
- **Tension / promise:** *my body is still shopping for two people.* No claim beyond the narrator's
  own habit; source = owner brief §User constraints (mandated line) + topic.
- **First concrete payoff (<8 s):** the printer finishes the `×2` receipt at **4.76 s**, and at
  **5.26 s** the hard cut lands on the toothbrush holder — one dry brush, one wet one, the same habit
  in a second object, already on screen before 8 s. The dry/wet sentence completes at 9.16 s.
- **Closing payoff:** s5–s6 reverse the same counter and the same receipt to `×1`, then the box is
  opened, eaten, and the last line refuses the uplift ("Chỉ thấy no."). The promise ("I still shop
  for two") is resolved by a visible act, not by advice.
- **Anti-clickbait risk:** none material — the line is the owner's, the image is the line, and the
  video delivers the reversal it implies. Risk is mild over-familiarity of the "chia tay" topic;
  countered by the specificity of the receipt and the refusal of a healing arc.

**Scores: S 10 · O 9 · F 10 · P 10 = 39/40 (9.75)**

## H2 — "Chị bán hàng hỏi: Hai hộp hả em?" (surprise / contrast · rejected)

- **First frame (0–1 s):** the vendor's hand pushes a second box across the steel; the narrator's
  hand is frozen above it, fingers open. The vendor is out of focus behind the steam.
- **On-screen text:** `"HAI HỘP HẢ EM?"` / `TÔI GẬT ĐẦU.` / `RỒI ĐỨNG IM.`
- **Spoken 0–3 s:** "Chị bán hàng hỏi: Hai hộp hả em? Tôi gật đầu. Rồi đứng im." — 14 syllables,
  ≈3.3 s. Sound off, the text and the two hands tell the whole beat; the *reason* for the freeze
  needs the next line.
- **Tension / promise:** an outside person names the habit before the narrator does; the body answers
  "yes" out of routine while the mind has not caught up. Source: the owner's specified scene
  (checkout counter, two boxes, hand hesitating) — the vendor's line is invented dialogue consistent
  with that scene, not a factual claim.
- **First payoff (<8 s):** the gulp of the freeze, then the narrator looks at the second box.
- **Closing payoff:** resolves to the same one-portion act, so `P` is high.
- **Why rejected:** it makes a stranger the engine of the realisation and puts the narrator in
  reaction, which slightly externalises the feeling the video is about. It also adds a speaking
  extra (casting, lip-sync implication) to a 7-shot video where she would appear once, and "rồi
  đứng im" is a beat the audience has seen in every breakup short. H1 keeps the confession inside
  the narrator and still gets the freeze for free at s4a. Not chosen.

**Scores: S 8 · O 8 · F 9 · P 9 = 34/40 (8.5)**

## H3 — "Tôi bỏ được ảnh, bỏ được áo." (curiosity gap · rejected)

- **First frame (0–1 s):** a bathroom shelf at eye level, hard side light: a toothbrush holder with
  two slots, one brush dry and furred with dust, one wet. A folded shirt and a photo frame face-down
  on the shelf below, out of focus.
- **On-screen text:** `BỎ ĐƯỢC ẢNH. BỎ ĐƯỢC ÁO.` / `CÁI GIÁ THÌ KHÔNG.`
- **Spoken 0–3 s:** "Tôi bỏ được ảnh, bỏ được áo. Cái giá đỡ hai bàn chải thì vẫn để nguyên." —
  17 syllables, ≈4.0 s. Sound off, the image + text are immediately legible.
- **Tension / promise:** *why can this one object not be thrown away?* That is a real curiosity gap
  and it is the most "sound-off" of the three.
- **First payoff (<8 s):** the answer would have to arrive early; in this script it does not — the
  next beat is the dining chairs, then the counter.
- **Closing payoff:** the video resolves "one portion for one person", not "why the holder stayed".
  `P` is the weak axis: the promise is never cashed, which is exactly the anti-clickbait failure the
  hook contract forbids ("a hook unrelated to the final payoff").
- **Why rejected:** it is a bathroom-object hook in a video whose device is a shop counter, and its
  question is answered by a different video (the one about keeping objects). Its first frame also
  reuses the ex's left-behind clothing: `thuong-hay-so-bat-dau-lai` already puts "áo khoác người ấy
  vắt trên ghế" in its cover and its s2 (`script.md:8`, `s2`), so H3's folded shirt would read as the
  same image in a different room. `tiec-nguoi-hay-nam-thang` does **not** use that image — the
  overlap is with `thuong-hay-so-bat-dau-lai` only. Not chosen.

**Scores: S 8 · O 9 · F 8 · P 6 = 31/40 (7.75)**

---

## Decision

**H1 is selected.** It has the highest total and no axis below 9, it is the owner's verbatim line, and its promise
is cashed by the same counter and the same receipt 25 s later. H2 is the strongest competitor and
would be the fallback if the director ever needs a non-mandated opening; its exact wording is kept
above so it can be reused without rewriting. H1's exact wording is preserved unchanged in
`voice.proposed.json` (`s1`) and in `script.md`.
