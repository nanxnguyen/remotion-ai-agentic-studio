## 5. Five hook candidates (Vietnamese openers, ≤ ~10 words)

Each entry: the opener · the fact/line it rests on · the gap it opens in the first 1–3 s · truthful? · real payoff by ~8 s?

1. **"Bạn từng nói mình sẽ học đàn. Rồi bạn đi làm."**
   · Rests on: S1, lines 1 + 3. · Gap: a promise stated flatly, then cut off by ordinary life — what happened between? · **Truthful** (both clauses are the user's own lines). · **Payoff by ~8 s:** yes — the song list and "Để khi nào rảnh" arrive right after.
2. **"Một buổi dọn nhà, bạn tìm thấy cuốn sổ ngày xưa."**
   · Rests on: S1, line 5. · Gap: a found object with no explanation — what is written in it, and why does it matter? · **Truthful.** · **Payoff by ~8 s:** yes — the song list and the old handwriting.
3. **"'Để khi nào rảnh.' Bạn đã nói câu này bao nhiêu lần?"**
   · Rests on: S1, line 4 + S5/S6 (the phrase as real audience language). · Gap: a direct question that makes the viewer answer privately in the first seconds. · **Truthful** (the phrase is verbatim in both the user's script and public usage). · **Payoff by ~8 s:** yes — the deferred guitar and the years moving on.
4. **"Chữ viết ngày xưa vẫn còn nguyên. Bạn quên nó rồi."**
   · Rests on: S1, line 6 (+ I3, "you forgot it" is an inference). · Gap: whose handwriting, and why "still intact" is said with weight. · **Truthful, with I3 flagged** as inference. · **Payoff by ~8 s:** partial — the notebook opens and the writer's identity lands.
5. **"Điều bạn từ bỏ không ồn ào. Nó chỉ lặng lẽ biến mất."**
   · Rests on: the topic line "âm thầm từ bỏ" + S1 lines 3–4. · Gap: an abstract claim that begs "what did I give up?". · **Truthful** (matches the brief's topic). · **Payoff by ~8 s:** weaker — needs the object to appear fast or the claim stays abstract.

**Top 2 (recommended):** #1 and #3.
- #1 is the most concrete and lowest-risk: both halves are the owner's own sentences, so frame 0 says nothing the video cannot prove, and it sets up the whole arc in 10 words.
- #3 converts the hook into a question the viewer answers about themselves; it is the strongest viewer-recognition opener, and the phrase is verified in both the owner's script (S1) and public usage (S5/S6).

Neither #1 nor #3 is clickbait: both open a question that the video genuinely answers by ~8 s and again in the ending.
Avoid a hook that promises "học đàn dễ" or "đổi đời" — nothing in S1 supports it.
