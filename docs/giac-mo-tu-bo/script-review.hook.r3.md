- **H2** rejected on timing (3.08–3.33 at ≤ 3.9) and reused at `s5b` — the deferral, which is the better use.

### 1.6 Verdict on the hook

**9.0.** Frame-0 text is complete and rate-independent; the spoken hook is inside 3 s at every planned rate and its

because the packet already names it as render-only.

## 5 · Weaknesses found even where the packet passes (non-blocking)

1. **`s4` — `hộp thư đầy` is the least sensory noun in the video** and sits at the start of the 9.2–13.8 s flat
   stretch, where the image is already two objects (a paper tray and a row of unread rows). Rewrite at the same 8
   syllables: **`Sáng nào cũng quẹt thẻ, khay đầy giấy.`** — the object in frame becomes the object in the mouth.
   Not a ticket: the line is `[ADD]` and the flatness is deliberate; this is the one place where the deliberate
   flatness costs a beat more than it buys.
2. **`s6` — `rồi tắt màn hình.` narrates the action the viewer is watching** (the side button press) instead of
   landing on the state the shot ends on (locked grey). Same 11 syllables: **`Ngón tay khựng trước nút Mua ngay,
   rồi màn hình tắt.`** The final word then names the image, and the anticlimax is carried by the picture rather
   than by a verb.
3. **`s12`–`s13` — the lanyard returns after the permission instead of before it.** `:264` settles the camera on
   the lanyard *"on `không còn muốn trở thành nhạc công`"*, i.e. mid-clause in a 13-syllable line; the object that
   answers the hook should already be in frame when the sentence starts, so the viewer sees the trade before they
   hear it. Owner-locked line (`L9`), so this is a staging fix, not a rewrite: bring the lanyard into the lower
   third at the **top** of `s12`, and start the off-camera note of `s13` **under** `cây đàn` rather than after it.
