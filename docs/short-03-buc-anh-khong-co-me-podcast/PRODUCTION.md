# Short 03 — Bức Ảnh Duy Nhất Không Có Mẹ / visual podcast

**Format:** voice-first emotional storytelling podcast short, with a recognizable stick-figure host beside a studio microphone and 16 separate illustrated memory cutaways. No false lip-sync claim.

**Opening question (0–5 s):** Hơn trăm bức ảnh… nhưng Linh không thấy mẹ.

**Plot:** Mother photographed every milestone, family meals and the graduation; Linh notices her absence, zooms into the graduation photograph and discovers the mother holding the camera in the glass reflection. Linh finds mother in the kitchen, takes her hand, and makes the final photograph with mother at the center.

**Theme:** The person who kept the memories should also be in the memories.

**Timing:** 40.4167 seconds, 60 FPS, 1080×1920, 16 shots, 8 separately recorded Vbee Thanh Long narration beats. The motion is deterministic. Music is an original piano score that ducks under speech.

**Visual system:** black #08090B, white #F5F5F4, gray #85878A, amber #EF9E69. Podcast host/mic persist in the lower third; photo memories occupy a larger inset panel. Every two shots use one natural spoken thought. Zoom into glass reflection only at the reveal.

**Technical checks:** every visual shot boundary, stream presence, source Vbee MP3 decoding, duration 30–45s, audio peak, FFmpeg sidechain smoke test, final QA JSON and checksum.

**Limitations:** Subjective naturalness of the speech and perceived emotion require actual user/human listening. The bespoke Pillow/FFmpeg render is not a claim of canonical Remotion pipeline gate A–C completion. Video is not automatically posted to YouTube.

**Delivery:** after AV QA, archive the MP4 on this feature branch and import the complete MP4 into the user's vidIQ library; record the durable upload ID but no expiring signed link.
