# Psychology evidence, integrity and framing gate

This is an **educational entertainment series**, not a substitute for a clinician. For each psychology claim used as a fact, verify with accessible reputable sources (academic papers, systematic reviews, APA/NIH/NHS/major clinical institutions). Cite source in production notes and YouTube description if factual claims matter. Record publication date and limitations; old/popular blog posts can be topic prompts but not sufficient scientific proof.

Classify each sentence:
- `SUPPORTED`: reasonably supported, accurately phrased within source scope.
- `QUALIFIED`: plausible but evidence limited, varied or context-specific. Use “đôi khi”, “có thể”, “trong một số hoàn cảnh” rather than “luôn”, “chắc chắn”, “chỉ cần nhìn là biết”.
- `EXAMPLE`: fictional illustrative behavior, explicitly not a general psychological law.
- `OPINION`: value stance or suggested communication practice, labeled as advice, not science.
- `UNVERIFIED`: hold or rewrite before publishing.

**Never** diagnose mental-health conditions from micro-expressions, messages, sleeping a lot or one interaction. No unsupported manipulation “hacks,” inaccurate gender essentialism, fear/shame tactics, false statistics, fake named psychology studies, or fake therapy/expert endorsements. Don't trivialize coercion/abuse, self-harm or severe mental distress. When health information is salient, offer a professional-help caveat proportionate to risk.

Use both **counterexample** and **viewer utility**: a gesture can have many causes; instead of guessing motives, invite a respectful conversation or propose a practical boundary phrase. Avoid pretending one tactic will work with everyone.

Minimum episode fact-check ledger:
```
claim: "..."
classification: SUPPORTED | QUALIFIED | EXAMPLE | OPINION | UNVERIFIED
source: real URL or DOI, or "not applicable—fiction"
supported_scope: what the source actually demonstrates
caveat: what a viewer could misunderstand
approved_wording: exact final Vietnamese line
```

Before script lock, compare claim text to script and subtitle list; keep evidence note internal or cite in description where warranted. When tools/web unavailable, make the story explicitly illustrative, state factual verification pending, or use safe non-empirical framing rather than inventing sources.
