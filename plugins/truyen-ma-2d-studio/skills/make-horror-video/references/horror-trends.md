# What top horror channels do (research snapshot, November 2026)

Use this to choose a format, a length, a title pattern and a topic. Numbers are snapshots (vidIQ, NexLev, creator databases, Nov 2026); re-check with vidIQ/web when planning a series, because trends move.

## Vietnamese horror channels (the market for Hẻm Chuyện Lạ)

| Channel | Format | What the data says |
|---|---|---|
| **MYSU – Thy Thanh Pham** (~1.38 M subs) | 50–70 min narrated "Chuyện Tâm Linh" episodes, numbered `[Tập 1997]`, short punchy 2–4 word titles (TRẢ NGHIỆP, VONG NHẬP XÁC CHẾT, BÙA CÁ LÓC) | ~8.5 M long-form views / 30 days; every episode lands 250–450 K. Consistency + length = the biggest watch-time machine in the niche. Themes: nghiệp/nhân quả, bùa ngải, vong, quỷ, làng quê, đồ cúng. |
| **Chuyện Ma Vũ Mập** (~676 K) | 1–3 h listener-submitted "chuyện ma có thật", one per day at 20:00 | ~3.3 M long views / 30 days. Daily schedule + audience-submitted stories. |
| **Truyện của Emma** (~273 K) | Author's own original stories with her own illustrations; 3–6 min singles + 12–22 min themed compilations; Shorts feed the long videos | Growing ~4 %/month. Breakouts: **"Con Lân Người"** (Sep 2026, 1.06 M, 42× channel average), "Ông Kẹ" 2.29 M, "Những đứa trẻ đã đi đâu – Chuyện Nội Kể" 490 K. Compilations: "Câu chuyện đằng sau những đồ vật" 1.78 M, "Trò chơi – nghi thức" 1.3 M, "Kinh dị trường học" 1.05 M. **Closest model for a 2D illustrated channel.** |
| **PKD** (~276 K) | 8–14 min explainers of dark mysteries, lists of 3/5/10, CAPS on key words | Hits: "5 tập phim hoạt hình bị mất" 1.79 M, "Thuyết âm mưu 5 phim hoạt hình" 1.47 M, "10 chuyện cổ tích không dành cho trẻ em" 1.3 M, "Dark web nguy hiểm thế nào" 1.12 M. Oct 2026 "Thực hư thánh vật sông Tô Lịch" (32 min) ran ~2,100 views/hour — local Vietnamese mysteries are hot. |
| Chuyện Âm Dương, Mọt Phá Án | 20–35 min true crime + tâm linh | Breakout flags in 2026; mix of vụ án + bí ẩn. |

Takeaways for Vietnamese:
1. **Long beats Shorts for money.** Vietnamese Shorts pay very little per view; the top earners are 10–60 min videos.
2. **Folk/village horror is the strongest single theme** (lân, Ông Kẹ, ma da, bùa, cây đa, giếng, đám ma, rằm tháng bảy). "Chuyện Nội/Bà Kể" framing adds warmth and authority.
3. **Themed compilations** ("Những chuyện kinh dị ở trường học", "…về đồ vật", "…về trò chơi – nghi thức") are the fastest watch-hour builders.
4. **Short, concrete, CAPS titles**: an object or place + a threat. No clickbait that the story does not pay off.

Title formula that dominates English animated horror: **"{N} True {Place/Situation} Horror Stories Animated"** and **"{Short Creepy Quote}…"** — the quote is a line from the story.

## Retention facts every script must respect
- Most videos lose 20–40 % of viewers in the first 15–30 s; YouTube stops recommending a video that bleeds there. The first 30 s are the hook (make a promise), the rest is keeping it (pay it off, open the next loop).
- Shorts: the first 1.5–3 s decide swipe-away. Visual hook + spoken hook must fire together on frame 1.
- On faceless videos change what is on screen every 5–10 s in long-form and every 2–4 s in Shorts (horror dread shots can hold longer only if the camera creeps and something in frame changes).
- The second-biggest drop-off is around the 50 % mark → put a pattern interrupt there (reveal, chapter, twist).
- Horror audiences binge: end screens pointing to the next story and series numbering lengthen sessions.

## Format menu (pick one per video)
| Format | Length | Structure | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short | 45–60 s | hook 0–2 s → setup → one escalation → scare/reveal ~70 % → twist → CTA 3–4 s | reach, testing premises, funnel to long |
| Single story | 8–15 min | cold open → setup → 3-step escalation → midpoint reveal → climax → twist ending | core growth video |
| Compilation | 15–25 min | cold open from story 3 → 3 (or 4) stories, each 4–7 min, strongest LAST, chapter cards | watch hours, series |
| Long tâm linh | 30–60 min | narrated episode; fewer, longer shots (6–15 s) with constant slow motion; scares every 5–8 min | monetisation, loyal audience |
| Explainer "giải mã" | 10–30 min | question hook → 3–5 items or one case → verdict | PKD-style mysteries; facts must be checked |
