# Folklore bank — premises with safe angles

Adapt folklore freely (it is public domain), but write your own story around it. Use the listed visual anchors with `horror.js` props.

## Vietnamese folk & village horror
| Legend / custom | Core fear | Fresh angle ideas | Visual anchors |

## Modern Vietnamese urban horror (Hẻm Chuyện Lạ house style)
Late-night bus (xe buýt số 0, 02:17), hotel room that doesn't exist (404), laundromat that receives tomorrow's clothes, apartment floor missing from the lift, dorm roll call with one extra name, hospital night shift, ride-hailing pickup at the cemetery, rented room with a sealed altar, the alley lamp that only flickers when someone is behind you. Keep places fictional ("một con hẻm ở quận cũ"), never a real business name or address.

## Cổ tích đen (dark retellings of public-domain tales)
Tấm Cám (the revenge cycle is already horror), Sọ Dừa, Thạch Sanh (the python's victims), Sự tích con muỗi, Chử Đồng Tử, Trương Chi (the cursed cup), Mỵ Châu – Trọng Thủy, Chàng Cóc. Global: Hamelin piper, Bluebeard, original Grimm. Style: `giayma`.

## English-language premises
Rules horror (night-shift job rules), liminal places, "3 true ___ stories" (camping, motel, babysitting, delivery driver, dating app, road trip), cryptids (skinwalker-style shapeshifters — avoid sacred Indigenous specifics; invent your own creature), analog horror tapes (`vhs` style), dark fairy tales.

## Never
- Real victims, real crimes presented as entertainment with invented details, or real addresses/businesses as haunted.
- Mocking a religion, ethnic group or a minority's sacred practices; keep customs respectful (explain them, don't ridicule).
- Self-harm or suicide shown or described as a method; if a story touches it, keep it off-screen and non-specific.
- Children in danger shown graphically; sexual content.
- Copying a story from another creator, Reddit, creepypasta or a book without permission.
