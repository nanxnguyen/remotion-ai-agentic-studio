# Story retention scorecard — editorial, not an official YouTube formula

Reference articles (as of Oct 2026):
- YouTube Blog, "YouTube Shorts deep dive", Jan 28, 2025: https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/ (first-second hook is crucial).
- YouTube Studio Shorts analytics: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217 (how many chose to view, views, watch time, etc.).
- YouTube analytics metrics: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281 (stayed to watch, engaged views, average percentage viewed; not all views equal continued attention).
- Audience retention: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415 (segment-level dips, watch again; observed AFTER publishing).

## Time contract

0–1s: arresting, emotionally legible frame, ideally active choice/action.
0–3/5s: the FULL hook; user default: **3–5 seconds**, not a fixed 2-second hook; keep question unresolved.
4–12s: two concrete memories/context moments, emotional attachment.
12–20s: visible drift, mounting silence, re-hook around 15–22s.
20–24s: new encounter/inciting situation; speed up tension.
24–33s: decisive beat / character action and emotionally specific revelation.
33–39s: interpret without preachiness.
39–45s: quiet satisfying resolution, a memorable line that may prompt natural sharing.

This is a creator's editorial structure, not a guaranteed recommendation ranking system. Tune to each story and audio.

## Mandatory 10-point creative audit (0, 1, or 2 each)

1. Hook's first visual intelligible sound-off; contradiction/action visible at <1s.
2. Hook spoken aloud plausibly fits 3–5s and opens a question, not an essay.
3. Protagonist faces a **specific relatable event**, not only philosophical generalization.
4. Stakes/contrast by second 10.
5. Each scene adds new information/emotion/action; no repeated exposition.
6. Re-hook/turn and climax arrive before audience fatigue.
7. Emotional behavior human and consistent; no cheap betrayal twist without setup.
8. Frame variation & camera continuity, silhouette recognizable on phone.
9. Last line adds reframe/healing, not an engagement beg.
10. Voice plausibly timed and captions/music contribute rather than mask.

Draft score is self-review only: 16–20 = editor-ready; 12–15 = revise weakest two dimensions; <12 = redesign premise. A high score isn't a predictor of a million views.

## Post-publish test

Check **Stayed to watch / chose to view**, **Engaged views**, average view duration, **Average percentage viewed**, retention dips at 0–5s, 15–22s and 25–35s; collect comments/shares and test alternative hooks against comparable posts. Benchmark to the channel's past Shorts of comparable topic/length rather than invent universal thresholds or pretend unpublished analytics exist. Views count changes over time; engaged views matter for depth.
