# Curiosity Intro + Emotional Retention + Follow CTA (45s)

Policy version: 1.0 — 2026-10-27. Applies to Ray Emotional Stick Shorts, Shorts to Remotion Studio, and new Vietnamese stick-figure narrative/motivation Shorts. This is the **default creative timeline**, not a hard YouTube duration rule.

## Default runtime and segment timing

- **Target 40–45 seconds (preferred default: 45 s)** for a 10-beat narrative with emotional climax, intro and follow CTA; avoid filler. For 15–35 s topics, compress proportionally, never force a story to exactly 45 s. Extend only when plot/voice pacing justify it and user asks.
- **0.0–2.0/3.0 s — Curiosity INTRO / soft opening CTA:** an intriguing second-person question or provocative promise about the story, not a generic channel ident. Example: “Bạn còn nhớ ước mơ đầu tiên không?” Open with the strongest action/image within frame 0. Never start with repetitive “Hôm nay chúng ta sẽ tìm hiểu về…”, unless the user specifically prefers that educational mode.
- **2.0/3.0–6.0 s — Story HOOK:** a concrete anomaly, emotional stake, or surprising incident; continue visual action from intro. For very strong story hooks, merge intro into hook to avoid two successive openings.
- **6–25 s — Development and re-hook:** 1–2 meaningful escalations, stakes and observable character action; show, don't only tell.
- **25–39 s — Climax:** a clearly filmed decision, reversal or act of courage; voice/music/SFX build and release naturally.
- **39–42 s — Residual emotional line:** succinct memorable payoff. Leave breathing space if earned.
- **42–45 s — End CTA (1–4 s):** story-specific invitation to follow/subscribe (“Nếu câu chuyện này tiếp thêm cho bạn can đảm… theo dõi kênh nhé.”). Keep mood; no aggressive overlay or chant. Loop-friendly exit frame.

## Cinematic and accessibility implementation

- Intro visuals: character turns to camera / reaches toward viewer / interacts with story prop; 0–3 s, no prolonged logo, large intro card or arbitrary SFX overload. Never sacrifice the first second of curiosity to announce the channel.
- End CTA: animated subscribe/follow hint (tap/ripple/soft motion) 1–4 s, no intrusive banner, and no abrupt loss of music or speech. Do not assert user subscribed; it is only an invitation.
- Captions: **max two lines**, mobile safe zone, clear Vietnamese font and diacritics (prefer Be Vietnam Pro ExtraBold if available), cream-white + restrained amber, thin dark outline/shadow, no opaque black ASS boxes by default. No decorative heading duplicating the spoken caption. Animated emphasis only where meaningful; if word-level alignment is requested, gate on actual verified word timestamps, not guessed word durations.
- Voice Vbee Thanh Long genuinely synthesized from approved narration; direct intro with curiosity, act with emotional contrast, and warm authentic CTA. Preserve music rights, audio ducking and story-specific emotional cues. Export H.264 + AAC stereo 48 kHz; inspect mobile visual frames and listen to the finished MP4.
- Character movement, shot variation, prop contact and continuity are independently checked. 30 fps or pixel-change statistics are not proof of natural movement.

## Mandatory review gate (before any new script/video)

1. Script and storyboard must explicitly label INTRO, HOOK, RE-HOOK, CLIMAX, PAYOFF and END CTA with non-overlapping timestamps totaling 40–45 s (or user-set runtime).
2. **Hook-first comparison**: make an A/B alternative without the intro and compare on actual viewer analytics where available; if intro delays the story or weakens retention, replace it with a blended intro/hook. No claims of improved retention without measurements.
3. Validate actual synthesized voice duration after adding both CTA lines; re-time 10 scenes/captions and music to the audio before export. No speed-up that hurts emotion solely to fit 45 s.
4. Check intro and CTA on 360×640 mobile preview; no occluded subtitles or UI conflicts; avoid interrupting the climax to demand subscribe.
5. Keep versions beside the original in the **same canonical numbered Videos-shorts Google Drive folder**, following the owner SOP; do not overwrite/delete files without explicit permission. For V5, store as versioned REVIEW until approved and independently QA'd.
