# CAPTIONED_STORYBOARD_REVIEW_V1 — Mandatory review design (2026-10-24)

**Reference:** user-supplied boards “Bí ẩn căn phòng 404” (4×4 portrait panels, white/yellow/red high-impact text in each) and “Bức ảnh thứ tư” (cinematic title banner + numbered 2-column film frames). **Every horror storyboard submitted for review MUST carry readable Vietnamese storytelling text on EVERY panel**, as in those references. A clean textless board may be additionally supplied as an asset, but it is **not** a substitute for the captioned review board.

## Visual and story treatment
- Film title with an arresting line is optional above board, especially for a 2-column design. Default Shorts board: 16 varied story-specific images in a 4×4 vertical-panel review mosaic; separate panels may alternatively be displayed 2 columns for longer captions. Keep consistent protagonist/age/wardrobe, use varied close/wide/POV/evidence/other character shots, moody cinema color and suspense escalation.
- **Every panel includes an actual story beat:** 1–2 concise Vietnamese lines (normally 5–14 words altogether; split longer beats to stay legible). Render as **native text in a layout engine** rather than asking image generation to spell it. Verify all diacritics. This text explains a reveal, clue, line or dramatic action, not generic category labels.
- Color semantics: **white/ivory** = narration, **amber/yellow** = reveal/evidence/high-value keywords, **crimson red** = danger/twist. Use bold condensed cinematic title weight, subtle shadow or low-gradient underlay, safe margins, mobile readability. Hero headline can be bigger and distressed. Do not overfill all panels with red.
- Reviewer may use small scene order/time marker or title in a **review-only** caption lane. Distinguish the reference's “00:00–00:03” stamps from actual MP4: **never burn them into final video**. Avoid debug banners, white board dividers or watermark in rendered film.
- Board review must be visually understandable without audio, with an emotional progression: **HOOK 0–6s; BODY 6–30s; CLIMAX 30–48s; PAYOFF 48–60s** for 60s Shorts. Number/timestamp scene labels only where helpful to review.
- Validate phone-scale text readability, Vietnamese spelling and caption-to-scene story correspondence; avoid repeating same face or generic “gương mặt sợ hãi” across all frames.

## Absolute separation from export footage
**Contact-sheet/storyboard with text is a REVIEW artifact.** Final MP4 scenes derive from clean individual image/video assets **without baked review caption/timecode/grid**. Build final kinetic Vietnamese subtitles as separate Remotion overlays synchronized to measured real Vbee speech, retaining cinematic color hierarchy. Never crop in leftover review-board captions from image sources or show 4×4 grid as footage. For a smooth cinematic 2D/2.5D FINAL, test no freeze/jitter and inspect rendered MP4 before archiving.

## Production acceptance
Storyboards missing visible Vietnamese explanatory/highlight text in one or more panels fail review delivery. Actual final MP4 with inherited storyboard badges/dividers fails video QA. These are separate gates, not contradictory.
