# Creative direction

## Design system
- Deep ink background #080B14, cyan #22D3EE, indigo #6366F1, warm off-white text.
- Type hierarchy: high-contrast headline 80–130px, concise supporting line 44–64px; test on phone.
- Use bold animated type on semantic beats; do not cover important diagrams with captions.

## Signature six-second hook
0–1.5s: app window slams into view; lock appears over source; subtle electronic hit.
1.5–3s: scanning ray reveals module tree and IPC arrows, not magically unlocked source; raise tempo.
3–4.5s: 'REA' title emerges through parallax and clean particle dispersal; bass accent.
4.5–6s: flow nodes connect (Agent → MCP → REA → tools → evidence); continuous camera move.

## Sound direction
Voice-first. Intro impact, low-volume electronic pulse, delicate transient per visual transition. Avoid constant whooshes and aggressive bass. Use automated ducking during narration and fade music on CTA.

## Comedy direction
- Build each joke as setup → micro-pause → visual payoff. Leave 6–12 frames of breathing room after a punchline when timing permits.
- Use reaction cuts, literalized analogies, freeze beats, restrained zooms or a single surprising UI change. The visual must clarify the joke and the product idea at the same time.
- Let the narrator underplay the funny line; avoid comedy sound effects on every sentence.
- Use one recurring visual motif as a callback. Do not rely on unrelated meme footage, excessive emojis or flashing text.

## Caption direction
2–6 words per cue, meaningful grouping. Highlight at most one keyword per cue. Maintain central safe zone; verify with phone-size preview. Always use actual speech-aligned cue timings.
Keep captions faithful to conversational pauses. Emphasize the punch word after the setup instead of displaying the entire joke at once.
